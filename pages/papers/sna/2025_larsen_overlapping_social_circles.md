# Overlapping social circles in historical elite career networks:Measuring accumulated social status with ‘k-circles’ and circle esteem in bipartite networks
**제목(한글)**: 역사적 엘리트 경력 네트워크의 중첩 사회적 원: 이원 네트워크에서 k-서클과 서클 명성을 통한 누적 사회적 지위 측정

**저자**: Anton Grau Larsen; Jacob Aagaard Lunding; Christoph Ellersgaard
**출처**: Social Networks, Vol.83, pp.28–49

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 덴마크 엘리트 30,000명 이상의 경력 데이터에서 k-서클(k-circles) 방법을 통해 사회적 지위 축적을 어떻게 측정할 수 있는가?

**방법론**:
- Breiger(1974)의 인물-집단 이중성 개념을 전기 데이터로 확장
- k-서클: 최소 구성원 분해(minimal members decomposition) 접근법
- 소속 네트워크 분석과 경력 네트워크 기반 k-서클 비교

**주요 결과**:
- k-서클은 엘리트 모임 지점이 아닌 경력 허브(career hub)를 식별
- 중심 인물들은 특권적 배경 출신이며, 중심 조직들은 경력 허브로 기능
- k-서클이 표준 중심성 지표와 갖는 관계 탐구

## 초록 (원문)

By extending Breiger’s (1974) notion of the duality of persons and groups to biographical data, individuals and organisations can be linked through career overlaps, formalised as k -circles, a minimal members decomposition approach. This k -circle measure is used to identify key individuals and organisations in biographies of more than 30,000 Danish elites between 1910 and 2020. We compare the central circle based on CV data to an elite identified through affiliation networks, showing that k -circles identify career hubs rather than the meeting points of elites. We discuss how k -circles are associated with standard measures of centrality. • Presents career networks as a way to assess capital accumulation over the organisational trajectories of elites which brings past ties into elite studies. • Proposed novel method, k -circles, for analysing career overlaps of elites to identify central actors and organisations. • Compares key organisations and individuals identified through membership network analysis and career networks based on k -circles. • Shows that central individuals come from privileged backgrounds and central organisations are career hubs.
