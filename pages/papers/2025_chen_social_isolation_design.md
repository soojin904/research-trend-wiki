# Social isolation by design: Bias in measuring core networks in Taiwan?
**제목(한글)**: 설계에 의한 사회적 고립: 대만 핵심 네트워크 측정의 편향?

**저자**: Minheng Chen; Yang-chih Fu; Xin Guo; Qiang Fu
**출처**: Social Networks, Vol.85, pp.1–12
**발행일**: 2025-11-21
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socnet.2025.11.001

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 대만의 핵심 토론 네트워크 규모가 1997~2017년 사이 변화했는가, 그리고 설문 설계 방식과 사회적 바람직성 편향(social desirability bias)이 이 측정에 어떤 영향을 미치는가?

**방법론**:
- 대만 사회변화 조사(Taiwan Social Change Survey) 데이터 활용
- 수정 포아송 혼합 회귀(modified Poisson mixture regression)로 핵심 네트워크 변화 분석
- 피셔 정보 최대화(Fisher Information Maximizer)로 이름 생성기(name generator) 효율성 평가

**주요 결과**:
- 사회적 바람직성이 높은 응답자는 핵심 연락처가 유의미하게 적고 사회적 고립 위험이 높음
- 이름 생성기는 설계 오류가 미미하며 정확한 열거 방식과 비슷한 추정치 도출
- 대면 설문 방식의 변화와 동아시아 문화적 맥락이 네트워크 측정에 영향을 줌

## 초록 (원문)

The estimation and measurement of the size of egocentric networks have sparked vigorous discussion and debate. Drawing on datasets from the Taiwan Social Change Survey, this study explores methodological issues pertaining to the change of core networks in Taiwan from 1997 to 2017 via a modified Poisson mixture approach, assesses the efficiency of name generators as a survey instrument via Fisher Information Maximizer, and investigates the role of social desirability in reporting core networks. Net of other effects, the study finds that individuals expressing a strong sense of social desirability report significantly fewer close contacts and face a higher risk of social isolation. Name generators in this study are associated with trivial design errors and can yield estimates comparable to those produced by exact enumeration. These findings are situated in the drastic changes in face-to-face survey interviews as well as the cultural context of Taiwan and, more broadly, East Asia. They call for further research inquiries into methodological issues regarding measuring and estimating egocentric networks in a transnational and modern setting. • This study shows changes in Taiwan’s core discussion networks from 1997 to 2017. • We use modified Poisson mixture regression and Fisher Information Maximizer. • High social desirability linked with fewer contacts and higher isolation risk. • Name generators show minimal design errors, comparable to exact enumeration. • It highlights cultural context and calls for transnational methodological research.
