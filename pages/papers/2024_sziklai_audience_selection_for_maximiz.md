# Audience selection for maximizing social influence

**제목(한글)**: 사회적 영향력 극대화를 위한 청중 선택

**저자**: Balázs R. Sziklai; Balázs Lengyel

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 영향력 극대화(IM) 문제와 달리 이질적 청중 집합의 사회적 영향력을 평가하는 청중 선택(AS) 문제에서 어떤 네트워크 중심성 지표가 가장 효과적인가?

**방법론**:
- 청중 선택(AS) 문제 정의 및 영향력 극대화(IM) 문제와의 차별점 제시
- 노드 샘플링 및 순위 차이 합산(Sum of Ranking Differences) 통계 방법 활용
- 두 개 온라인 소셜 네트워크에서 선형 임계값 확산 모형 적용, 8개 중심성 지표 평가

**주요 결과**:
- 저영향력 개인이 포함될 때 청중 선택 문제에서 중심성 지표의 통계적 평가 결과가 IM 문제와 현저히 다름
- 청중 선택 문제는 집합 단위 평가가 필요하고 일반 행위자의 기여도까지 측정해야 함
- 마케팅 이벤트 장소 선택 등 실용적 응용에 적합한 프레임워크 제공

## 초록 (원문)

Abstract Viral marketing campaigns target primarily those individuals who are central in social networks and hence have social influence. Marketing events, however, may attract diverse audience. Despite the importance of event marketing, the influence of heterogeneous target groups is not well understood yet. In this paper, we define the Audience Selection (AS) problem in which different sets of agents need to be evaluated and compared based on their social influence. A typical application of Audience selection is choosing locations for a series of marketing events. The Audience selection problem is different from the well-known Influence Maximization (IM) problem in two aspects. Firstly, it deals with sets rather than nodes. Secondly, the sets are diverse, composed by a mixture of influential and ordinary agents. Thus, Audience selection needs to assess the contribution of ordinary agents too, while IM only aims to find top spreaders. We provide a systemic test for ranking influence measures in the Audience Selection problem based on node sampling and on a novel statistical method, the Sum of Ranking Differences. Using a Linear Threshold diffusion model on two online social networks, we evaluate eight network measures of social influence. We demonstrate that the statistical assessment of these influence measures is remarkably different in the Audience Selection problem, when low-ranked individuals are present, from the IM problem, when we focus on the algorithm’s top choices exclusively.
