# A general model for how attributes can reduce polarization in social groups
**제목(한글)**: 속성이 사회 집단의 양극화를 어떻게 완화할 수 있는지에 대한 일반 모형

**저자**: Piotr J. Górski; Curtis Atkisson; Janusz A. Hołyst
**출처**: Network Science, Vol.11, pp.536–559
**발행일**: 2023-07-24
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1017/nws.2023.13

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 개인의 속성(인종, 재산, 정치적 성향 등)이 네트워크에서 구조적 균형과 양극화를 어떻게 억제할 수 있으며, 속성 유형(연속형, 서열형, 이진형)에 따라 효과가 어떻게 다른가?

**방법론**:
- 구조적 균형(structural balance) 기반 양극화 모형에 속성 변수 통합
- 연속형, 서열형, 비서열형, 이진형 등 5가지 속성 클래스 분석 프레임워크 개발
- 시뮬레이션으로 양극화 예방 및 탈양극화 효과 비교

**주요 결과**:
- 연속형 속성이 양극화 예방과 탈양극화 모두에서 가장 효과적
- 이진형 속성이 가장 효과적이지 않으며, 서열형이 그 중간
- 커뮤니티가 있는 네트워크 구조에서 양극화 해소에 국소적 긴장 유발이 필요할 수 있음

## 초록 (원문)

Abstract Polarization makes it difficult to form positive relationships across existing groups. Decreasing polarization may improve political discourse around the world. Polarization can be modeled on a social network as structural balance, where the network is composed of groups with positive links between all individuals in the group and negative links with all others. Previous work shows that incorporating attributes of individuals usually makes structural balance, and hence polarization, harder to achieve. That work examines only a limited number and types of attributes. We present a generalized model and a simulation framework to analyze the effect of any type of attribute, including analytically as long as an expected value can be written for the type of attribute. As attributes, we consider people’s (approximately) immutable characteristics (e.g., race, wealth) and such opinions that change more slowly than relationships (e.g., political preferences). We detail and analyze five classes of attributes, recapitulating the results of previous work in this framework and extending it. While it is easier to prevent than to destabilize polarization, we find that usually the most effective at both are continuous attributes, followed by ordered attributes and, finally, binary attributes. The effectiveness of unordered attributes varies depending on the magnitude of negative impact of having differing attributes but is smaller than of continuous ones. Testing the framework on network structures containing communities revealed that destroying polarization may require introducing local tensions. This model could be used by policymakers, among others, to prevent and design effective interventions to counteract polarization.
