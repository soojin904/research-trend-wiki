## 초록 (원문)

Although handwritten prescriptions are still frequently used in medical practice, automated recognition is severely hampered by their irregular spacing, variety of handwriting styles, and use of non-standard abbreviations. Current OCR techniques frequently rely on printed text, are insufficiently scalable to handle recently approved drug names, and are restricted to small or single-language datasets. The main research question arises from this gap: How can a multilingual, scalable, and highly accurate framework for the trustworthy recognition of handwritten medical prescriptions be developed? To close this gap, we suggest a hybrid deep learning OCR system that combines multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) and convolutional neural network (CNN) layers. Through GPU-accelerated mini-batch training, the model can be incrementally adjusted, is computationally efficient, and generalizes to various handwriting styles. The framework provides a reliable, scalable, and extremely accurate method for integrating handwritten prescriptions into electronic health records (EHR) systems through digitization. It was trained on a large dataset that contained medical, numerical, and textual elements.
