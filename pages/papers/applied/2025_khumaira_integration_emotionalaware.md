# Integration of an Emotional-Aware Recommender System Based on SBERT and Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis for Korean Skincare Product Recommendations

**저자**: Khairunida Khumaira; Z.K.A. Baizal
**출처**: , Vol.None, pp.551-556
**발행일**: 2025-12-17
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1109/icicyta68677.2025.11362833

## 초록 (원문)

The proliferation of Korean skincare products on e-commerce platforms has increased customer choice, but it has also made it more difficult to determine which products are best for a given person's skin type and preferences. The ability of current recommender algorithms in this field to capture complex user viewpoints and emotional undertones revealed in reviews is limited since they frequently depend solely on product popularity or cursory text analysis. Furthermore, this work proposed a hybrid user review-based system in a multi-stage manner to overcome this difficulty. First, the semantic links between sentences are modeled using Sentence-BERT. Subsequently, Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis focusing on texture and absorption is conducted using a fine-tuned DeBERTa model. Furthermore, the identification of positive, negative, and none emotional expressions, such as joy and regret, is performed using a RoBERTa-based model. System performance was evaluated using precision, recall, and nDCG metrics at Top-1, Top-3, and Top- 5 rankings, and compared against a baseline SBERTonly model. The proposed hybrid model has better results than SBERT-only model, and did best with a precision@1 of 0.6370, a recall@5 of 0.7261, and a nDCG@ 5 of 0.7240, compared to the SBERT-only model which produces the best performance with a precision@1 of 0.1344, a recall@5 of 0.3286, and a nDCG@5 of 0.2360. The findings show that as <tex xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink">$K$</tex>-values increases, precision decreases, while recall and nDCG scores increase. These findings show that incorporating semantic analysis, aspect-level sentiment, and emotional signals makes the recommendations significantly more valuable.
