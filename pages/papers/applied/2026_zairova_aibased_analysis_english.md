# AI-BASED ANALYSIS OF ENGLISH EMOTIONAL-STATE VERBSIN LITERARY DISCOURSE

## 초록 (원문)

The article examines the semantic and contextual features of verbs expressing emotional states in English in literary discourse and the possibilities of their analysis using artificial intelligence. The study summarizes lexicon-based, machine learning, and hybrid approaches used in the automatic analysis of emotional lexicon, and evaluates the capabilities of modern NLP and AI models in detecting emotional units, semantic classification, frequency of processing, and determining contextual features. In literary texts, this concept is further expanded, because here the author's style, the characters’ speech, narrative strategies, and the reader’s interpretive activity interact with each other. The difficulties posed by ambiguity, metaphor, irony, and indirect emotional expression in literary discourse in automatic analysis are particularly emphasized. It is determined that although AI is an effective tool in the systematic analysis of emotional verbs, human verification of the results obtained from linguistic and philological perspectives is necessary. Expressing emotions through language is a pressing issue in modern linguistics. It has been established that in literary discourse, emotional meaning is formed not only by the semantics of individual words, but also by the interaction of narrative context, the character’s psychological state, and the author’s stylistic choices. Verbs expressing emotional states have been shown to play a significant role in representing the characters’ inner worlds and constructing the emotional-semantic structure of the text. The study shows that verbs expressing emotional states play an important role in shaping the internal state of characters and the emotional-semantic structure of the text in literary discourse. Since the meaning of these verbs depends on the context, the lexical-semantic approach in their analysis should be complemented by discursive and contextual analysis.
