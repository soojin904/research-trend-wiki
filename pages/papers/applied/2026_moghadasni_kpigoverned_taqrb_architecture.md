# A KPI-Governed Taqrīb Architecture: A Data-Driven Roadmap to the New Islamic Civilization (DDRNIC)

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 이슬람 분파 간 화합과 협력을 뜻하는 규범적 타크리브(Taqrīb) 이념을 어떻게 실증적으로 측정하고, 지속적으로 통제 가능한 데이터 기반의 문명적 로드맵으로 전환할 것인가?

**방법론**:
- 타크리브 균형성과표(T-BSC)와 6대 핵심 관점(정체성과 사회적 결속, 지식과 대화, 번영과 복원력 등)에 맞춘 110개 KPI 사전 개발
- 설문 조사, 학술 성명서 및 미디어 텍스트 분석, 소셜 네트워크 분석(SNA) 융합
- 이중 차분법(DiD) 및 단절적 시계열 분석(ITS)을 결합한 준실험 평가 수행

**주요 결과**:
- 고도화된 데이터 관리 거버넌스가 분파 간 대화 강도(Dialogue Intensity) 및 화합 인식 인덱스를 직접적으로 향상시키는 선행 지표임을 규명함
- 기부 자산(Waqf)의 디지털 전환이 분파를 초월한 인도적 공동 프로젝트 유치로 이어진다는 인과 경로를 최초로 실증 평가함


## 초록 (원문)

This study engineers a KPI-governed taqrīb architecture (DDRNIC) to convert normative Islamic commitments into auditable civilizational progress. Using a design-science, mixed-methods approach, we develop the Taqrīb Balanced Scorecard (T-BSC) and a 110-KPI dictionary organized across six core perspectives: (1) Identity &amp; Social Cohesion, (2) Knowledge &amp; Dialogue, (3) Shared Prosperity &amp; Resilience, (4) Empowerment &amp; Equity, (5) Justice &amp; Just Peace, and (6) Governance &amp; Digital Stewardship which operationalize ten underlying strategic dimensions (e.g., Faith &amp; Identity; Scholarly Dialogue; Comparative Jurisprudence &amp; Fatwa Governance; Education; Women &amp; Youth; Media–Family; Cooperative Economics &amp; Waqf/Zakat; Peace, Security &amp; Justice; Sanctities Protection; Governance, Data &amp; Digital Maturity). Methods integrate structured surveys, document/content analysis of communiqués, fatāwā, and media outputs, social-network analysis, and quasi-experimental evaluation (A/B tests, difference-in-differences (DiD), interrupted time-series (ITS). Findings show that strong data stewardship (consent/privacy compliance, auditability) consistently precedes gains in Dialogue Intensity and the Unity Recognition Index; waqf digitization is associated with more bridge projects and higher Humanitarian Joint Action; and codified adab al-ikhtilāf plus media-literacy interventions reduce insult incidence and strengthen family-narrative resilience. Contributions include a portable governance blueprint, reproducible KPI definitions and formulas, and a reporting cadence for interoperable dashboards allowing ministries, universities, faith councils, and civil society to move from declarations to measurable, reproducible outcomes while preserving plurality through ethics-anchored safeguards.
