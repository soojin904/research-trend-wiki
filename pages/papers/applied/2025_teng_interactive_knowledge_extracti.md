# Interactive Knowledge Extraction from Textual Data Using Sentence Importance and User Feedback

**저자**: Rui Teng; Bin Liu; Yue Wu
**출처**: , Vol.None, pp.506-510
**발행일**: 2025-12-12
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1145/3788149.3788246

## 초록 (원문)

Rapid extraction of personally desired knowledge from textual data is important for learning and education. A user may takes time to understand and extract knowledge from a textual data. Although there are various studies on text-based recommendation systems and text analysis methods, few of these studies focus on the challenging issues of interactive and fast knowledge extraction on the basis of both text information and user understanding. This paper presents a novel interactive method for personalized knowledge extraction and recommendation from textual data. Compared with machine-learning based artificial intelligence, the proposed method does not require data training but has clear steps and explainable rules. The core innovation lies in ranking sentences based on their importance, dynamically adjusted through user interaction. The algorithm initially assesses sentence significance using word and sentence weights derived from frequency analysis and keyword prioritization. A key feature is the iterative refinement of these weights based on user queries, allowing the system to adapt to individual information needs. This approach combines statistical techniques with interactive feedback, offering a personalized and efficient means of knowledge discovery.
