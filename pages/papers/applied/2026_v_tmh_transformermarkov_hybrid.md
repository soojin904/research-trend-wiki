## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 비통제적인 실제 상황에서 소셜 미디어 내 다중 모드(텍스트, 오디오, 비디오) 데이터를 활용하여 우울증 및 감성 상태를 정확하게 탐지할 수 있는 효율적인 딥러닝 모델을 설계할 수 있는가?

**방법론**:
- 하이브리드 빨판상어 최적화 기법(hybrid remora optimization, HRO) 기반의 다중 모드 딥러닝 접근법(MDA-HRO) 설계
- 비디오, 오디오, 텍스트 피처 추출 기술 및 하이브리드 최적화 심층 신경망(CNN)의 결합
- D-Vlog 및 D-Reddit 데이터셋을 활용한 성능 평가

**주요 결과**:
- 제안된 MDA-HRO 프레임워크가 다중 모드 전반에서 감성을 효과적으로 예측함을 확인
- 각 데이터 모달리티별로 텍스트 98%, 오디오 97%, 비디오 96%의 매우 높은 정확도 달성
- 전통적인 분석 기법 대비 효율성을 높이고 다양한 다중모드 감성 분석 태스크에 대한 확장 가능성을 입증


## 초록 (원문)

Social media has emerged as a key platform in which people convey their opinions, emotions, and daily lives, with most of them showing cryptic indicators of mental disorders like depression. Detection of depression based on nonverbal behaviour has attracted significant attention. It is difficult to implement prior work on detecting depression in real-life situations since it was mainly focused on detecting depressed individuals in controlled laboratory environments. Depression reaches millions globally, and thus early and precise detection for timely intervention is essential. Diagnostic methods tend to be subjective and time-consuming based on the conventional methods. This paper proposes a multi-modal deep learning approach with hybrid remora optimization techniques (MDA-HRO) to integrate multi-modal data (video, audio, and text) to increase performance in emotional state of depression and sentiments. By combining the strengths of feature extraction techniques and a deep convolutional neural network optimized with hybrid optimization, the proposed framework effectively predicts sentiment across the modalities. The model is evaluated on the D-Vlog and D-Reddit datasets, achieving impressive accuracy rates for text data with 98%, audio data with 97%, and video data with 96%. Compared to conventional methods, the proposed work is more efficient and supports its applicability to various multimodal sentiment analysis tasks.
