# Comparing LDA and BERTopic for Topic Modeling of Skin Complaints in Indonesian-Language Sunscreen Reviews on Shopee

**저자**: Fahmi Ilmiah; Arif Faizin
**출처**: Journal La Multiapp, Vol.7, pp.983-998
**발행일**: 2026-10-05
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.37899/journallamultiapp.v7i4.5185

## 초록 (원문)

Sunscreen use in Indonesia continues to grow alongside the high ultraviolet exposure typical of a tropical climate. However, various complaints such as a sticky texture, white cast, and the appearance of acne are still frequently found in user reviews. These reviews contain important information about users' experiences and skin problems, yet they are difficult to analyze manually because of their large volume and unstructured nature. This study therefore aims to identify skin-complaint patterns in sunscreen use through an analysis of user reviews on Shopee Indonesia. It applies a text mining approach using topic modeling methods, namely Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) and BERTopic. Sunscreen product reviews were collected from the Shopee platform and then passed through preprocessing stages comprising cleaning, case folding, tokenizing, normalization, and stemming. The two methods were subsequently used to extract topics representing user complaints. Evaluation was carried out using the coherence score, topic diversity, and manual interpretation to assess the quality of the resulting topics. Based on the results, LDA produced nine topics with a coherence score of 0.35 and a topic diversity of 0.70, whereas BERTopic produced twelve topics with a coherence score of 0.50 and a topic diversity of 0.90. Based on the evaluation and an examination of dominant words and representative reviews, the topics produced by BERTopic showed better interpretability because they were more specific and easier to label, whereas LDA produced more general topics that still combined several complaints within a single topic.
