# Analysis of a Rumour Spread Model in Social Media Networks

**저자**: Abiodun Opanuga; Olasunmbo Agboola; Godliness Oloyede
**출처**: , Vol.None, pp.1476-1482
**발행일**: 2026-07-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1201/9781003782254-161

## 초록 (원문)

In the age of digital communication, online social networks have transformed the way information is disseminated, enabling rapid sharing and interaction among users while this connectivity fosters collaboration and knowledge exchange, it also facilitates the spread of misinformation and rumours which can have detrimental effects on society. This paper examines the dynamics of rumour transmission in social networks (LiveJournal) using the Susceptible-Infected-Recovered model. The model was analysed using fourth-order Runge-Kutta and differential transform methods. We examine how multiple parameters—specifically the stifling rate, spreading rate, and forgetting rate—affect rumour influence across varying degrees. Through simulation, it was observed that an increase in stifling rate and forgetting rate reduces the total rumour influence. It was also observed that increasing spreading rate increases rumour spread and reduces average threshold degree of rumour spread. Graphical illustrations of the rumour spread dynamics are presented in 2D form. Our findings illuminate how parameter adjustments can influence information spread in social networks.
