# Reconstructing nineteenth-century translator networks

**저자**: James St. André
**출처**: Benjamins translation library, Vol.None, pp.190-218
**발행일**: 2026-10-12
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1075/btl.172.08sta

## 초록 (원문)

Abstract This paper outlines the construction of a database and visualizations for Chinese-English translator networks in the nineteenth century. After discussing methodological problems that needed to be overcome to construct the database, the study highlights the advantages of a social network analysis approach for translation studies. The project demonstrates that Chinese-English translators in the nineteenth century were highly interconnected, with particularly dense clustering within two groups (missionaries and civil servants), but ties also cut across groups, showing a tight-knit community of sinological experts. The network highlights the potential of SNA in translation history, especially when combined with bibliometric and demographic data, revealing novel connections and patterns not apparent in earlier case studies, and identifying topics for further detailed qualitative analysis.
