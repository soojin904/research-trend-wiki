# Sentiment Orientation Classification of Chinese Internet Public Opinion Analysis on the Stock Market Based on Baidu NLP

**저자**: Zhang Xiaolan
**출처**: Systems and Soft Computing, Vol.None, pp.200487-200487
**발행일**: 2026-07-01
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sasc.2026.200487

## 초록 (원문)

The stock market often serves as a reflection of a country's overall competitiveness. With the rapid advancement of internet technology, an increasing number of stock investors are sharing their opinions on stocks through online platforms. The emotional tendencies expressed in the vast array of stock reviews may influence the stock market by shaping public opinion trends. Therefore, it is valuable to analyze investor comments from mainstream online platforms, conduct sentiment analysis, and develop a sentiment index to study its correlation with actual market performance. The stock-related public opinion data collected from East Money.com. was utilized in this study. Initially, the data was crawled using the requests library, followed by parsing with the Beautiful Soup library to extract the necessary information, which was then saved to a file. Subsequently, Baidu NLP's sentiment analysis API was employed to assess the sentiment orientation of the stock comments. The results demonstrate that the sentiment analysis achieved through this method outperforms traditional Naive Bayes-based sentiment analysis, with an error margin within 2% compared to manual analysis.
