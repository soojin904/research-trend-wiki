# Predicting Mental Health Risk Using Social Media Data and NLP

## 초록 (원문)

The increase in rates of psychological illnesses like depression, anxiety, and suicidal thoughts has made it essential that they are detected early and scalable detection systems also exist.With social media going everywhere, people spend increasingly more hours expressing their thoughts and feelings over the internet and hence express implicit signs of mental distress.This study investigates applying Natural Language Processing (NLP) methods for mental health risk prediction based on text analysis of social media user-content shared on Twitter and Reddit.We are employing linguistic features, sentiment patterns, and contextual cues to identify individuals at risk and classify mental health conditions with high sensitivity.The project entails using both classical machine learning methods and novel deep learning architecture such as transformer models like BERT and RoBERTa.We also face some of the major challenges such as data imbalance, reliability of annotations, ethical issues, and interpretability of models.The suggested approach shows promising performance in extracting mental health signals from raw text and indicates the promise of NLP-facilitated tools to aid digital mental health interventions.The research adds to the increasing body of work in computational mental health and serves as a basis for creating responsible, data driven mental health monitoring systems.
