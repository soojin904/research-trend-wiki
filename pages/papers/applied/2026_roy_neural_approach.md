## 초록 (원문)

Emotion recognition from textual data plays a vital role in enhancing human-computer interaction, sentiment monitoring, and mental health analysis. This manuscript proposes a robust deep learning framework for multi-class emotion detection using a Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) architecture integrated with word embeddings. By leveraging the contextual understanding capabilities of BiLSTM networks and the semantic richness of pre-trained word vectors, the proposed model effectively captures the sequential dependencies and subtle nuances in textual expressions. The dataset, comprising labelled text samples across multiple emotional categories, is rigorously pre-processed and encoded into dense representations. Extensive experiments demonstrate the model’s ability to generalize across diverse emotion classes, achieving high accuracy and balanced performance in precision, recall, and F1-score metrics. Comparative evaluations with baseline classifiers validate the superiority of the proposed approach in handling complex emotional contexts. This study underscores the potential of neural architectures in advancing real-time emotion-aware systems for applications in conversational AI, behavioural analytics, and affective computing.
