## 초록 (원문)

Analysing linguistic patterns in agricultural texts has become essential for effective knowledge management and information retrieval in the agricultural domain. Agricultural research and practice generate vast amounts of textual data containing valuable insights, making systematic analysis crucial for advancing agricultural knowledge systems. However, current approaches to agricultural text analysis often lack structured methodologies for identifying and extracting key themes and terminology patterns. While basic natural language processing techniques exist, they are typically generic and fail to capture domain-specific nuances in agricultural discourse. To address this gap, the study presents a computational framework specifically designed for agricultural text analysis, incorporating specialized tokenization, domain-aware stop word filtering, and agricultural terminology frequency analysis. This approach combines traditional NLP techniques with agricultural domain knowledge to generate visualizations of the top 20 most frequent terms, enabling the identification of dominant themes in agricultural texts. This methodology demonstrates significant improvements in extracting meaningful patterns from agricultural documents compared to generic text analysis tools, with potential applications in agricultural research trend analysis, knowledge base development, and specialized information retrieval systems. The framework’s ability to uncover domain-specific linguistic patterns provides valuable support for evidence-based decision-making in agricultural research and practice.
