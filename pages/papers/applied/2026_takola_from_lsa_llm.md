## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 빠르게 증가하는 생물다양성 및 생태 보존 분야 문헌으로부터 정책 수립에 유용한 의미 토픽을 추출하기 위해 전통적 확률론적 모델(LSA/LDA)과 임베딩 기반 모델(BERTopic), 최신 생성형 LLM 기법의 상대적 장단점은 무엇인가?

**방법론**:
- 2010년 이전부터 현재까지 발표된 생태 환경 보존 문헌을 표본으로 LSA, LDA, BERTopic, TopicGPT 등 대표 토픽 모델 기술 추이 고찰
- 정책 텍스트 및 학술 논문 속 생태 연구 공백 식별 능력 비교
- 각 인공지능 모델별 결과의 재현 가능성(Reproducibility)과 환경 비용(Environmental cost, 연산 전력) 요인 비교 분석

**주요 결과**:
- 전통 확률 모델은 분석 투명성과 매개변수 통제가 용이하나, 최신 LLM이 비정형 문헌 요약과 프롬프트 기반 주제 해석에서 더 유연함을 보임을 규명
- 다만 AI 모델의 높은 에너지 비용과 블랙박스 한계를 지적하고, AI 분류 도구는 현장 생태 지식과 전문가의 검수를 보조하는 용도로 보완 적용되어야 함을 제시


## 초록 (원문)

Biodiversity conservation faces mounting challenges due to accelerating species loss driven by habitat degradation, agricultural expansion, and climate change. Scientific literature grows rapidly, making automated text analysis tools increasingly essential for synthesizing knowledge and informing policy. This study reviews the evolution of topic modelling techniques—from Latent Semantic Analysis (LSA) and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) to embedding-based models like BERTopic and Top2Vec, and most recently, Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT. While traditional probabilistic models remain valuable for identifying thematic structures in ecological texts, LLMs offer enhanced flexibility, particularly for grey literature and prompt-based analysis. Applications of topic modelling in biodiversity conservation include identifying knowledge gaps, monitoring public perceptions, analysing policy texts, and summarising scientific literature. Despite recent advances, key challenges persist, including lack of domain-specific models, limited access to ecological corpora, and reproducibility concerns with AI models. Furthermore, the environmental cost of large-scale computation must be weighed against ecological goals. This review highlights that while AI-based topic models present valuable tools for ecological research and evidence synthesis, their role should remain complementary to human expertise and field-based knowledge. • Techniques have progressed from classical linear algebra methods like LSA to probabilistic models (LDA, STM) and embedding-based or generative models such as BERTopic, Top2Vec, and TopicGPT. • Topic modelling supports literature synthesis, stakeholder engagement, policy analysis, monitoring conservation projects, and identifying emerging threats or knowledge gaps. • Despite increasing use, topic modelling is still relatively rare in biodiversity research, especially before 2010, indicating untapped potential for guiding evidence-based conservation strategies. • While LLMs enable more flexible topic discovery, they have limitations, require human oversight, have high energy costs, and lack transparency compared to traditional probabilistic models. • AI-based topic models enhance research efficiency and insight extraction but cannot replace field knowledge, address systemic biodiversity data gaps, or substitute for evidence-based conservation action.
