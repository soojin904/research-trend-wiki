# ResearchMate AI: An NLP-Based Research Paper Analysis and Study Assistance System

**저자**: Abeeha Zeeshan

## 초록 (원문)

ResearchMate AI is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help students, researchers, and academic writers process large amounts of information more efficiently. The platform enables users to upload research papers, articles, and academic documents, automatically generate concise summaries, extract key insights, and identify important concepts within the text. Built using Python and Streamlit, ResearchMate AI combines natural language processing techniques with automated text analysis to reduce the time required to review lengthy documents. The system is designed to improve research productivity by helping users quickly understand complex material, locate relevant information, and organize knowledge for academic and professional work. The project demonstrates the practical application of artificial intelligence and natural language processing in educational technology, providing an accessible tool for literature review, document analysis, and research support.
