- 학술적 이동성(academic mobility) 지원에 적합한 설명 가능하고 신뢰성 높은 교육 프로그램 하이브리드 추천 개념 모델을 성립함


## 초록 (원문)

With the increasing number of international applicants interested in obtaining higher education in the Russian Federation, the need for convenient and personalized digital tools for selecting universities and academic programs is steadily growing. The aim of this study is to develop a conceptual model of an intelligent recommendation system designed to automate the process of selecting educational programs at Russian universities for foreign applicants. The proposed system is based on a hybrid architecture that integrates collaborative and content-based filtering methods, semantic text analysis models (Sentence-BERT), and an Explainable AI mechanism to justify the recommendations provided. The results of this research can be applied in the development of national educational platforms and university services supporting international academic mobility.
