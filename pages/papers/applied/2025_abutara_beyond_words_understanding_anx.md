# Beyond words: understanding anxiety and depression in college applicants through LIWC analysis of textual features

**제목(한글)**: 단어를 넘어서: LIWC 텍스트 특성 분석을 통한 대학 입시생의 불안·우울 이해

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 에세이의 언어적 특성이 입시생의 불안·우울 수준과 어떤 연관성을 갖는가?

**방법론**:
- 횡단면 연구 설계
- LIWC(Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count) 소프트웨어로 에세이 분석
- GAD-7(불안)·PHQ-9(우울) 척도 사용
- 다변량 회귀 모형으로 언어 특성과 점수 간 관계 분석

**주요 결과**:
- 높은 불안 수준: 소속·가정 관련 단어 증가, 여가·금전 관련 단어 감소
- 높은 우울 수준: 동기·숫자 관련 단어 증가, 움직임 관련 단어 감소
- 언어 분석이 교육 및 정신건강 맥락에서 조기 선별 도구로 활용 가능

## 초록 (원문)

Background: Anxiety and depression are highly prevalent among pre-university students, often intensified by the academic stress associated with entrance exams. Linguistic analysis of written texts offers a promising, non-invasive approach for early detection and prevention. Study design: Cross-sectional study. Objectives: To examine the association between linguistic features in essays and levels of anxiety and depression, identifying specific language patterns linked to these conditions. Setting: Pre-university preparatory courses in São Paulo, Brazil, in 2023. Methods: = 20.3 years, SD = 2.65) who completed a self-report form shared via WhatsApp or in-person at preparatory schools. The form included sociodemographic questions, the GAD-7 and PHQ-9 scales, and the upload of an argumentative essay written within the previous month as part of their regular coursework. Essays were analyzed using LIWC software, and multivariate regression models identified linguistic features associated with anxiety and depression scores. Results: Higher anxiety levels correlated with increased use of words related to affiliation and home, and decreased use of leisure and money-related terms. Depression was associated with higher frequency of drives and number-related words, and fewer motion-related terms. Conclusion: Linguistic analysis can assist in identifying emotional distress among pre-university students, offering a potential tool for early screening and intervention in educational and mental health contexts.
