# A Multimodel AI Framework for Stress Detection Using Voice and Text Analysis

**저자**: R. Abinayan; L. Thara; V. S. Anita Sofia
**출처**: International Journal of Scientific Research in Science Engineering and Technology, Vol.13, pp.262-270
**발행일**: 2026-10-11
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.32628/ijsrset2613522

## 초록 (원문)

Stress plays a role in affecting focus levels, work efficiency, mental health status as well as overall wellbeing. Traditional methods of measuring stress usually involve surveys, interviews, or specialized sensors that are not easily available for continuous use by people. The article is about an AI system that detects stress through speech and textual data collection methods. A one dimensional CNN is used by this model for extracting local acoustic characteristics of audio signals through its use of bidirectional LSTM layers that can be applied on time series data such as spectrograms or mel-spectrograms. In addition to this, we use an existing pre-trained Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT) model which is trained on data of stress classification through Dredit datasets. Cross attention is used to combine audio features and textual data for a single model during multimodal classification tasks. According to this report of benchmark evaluation, a voice model has an accuracy rate of 78.18%, precision at 76.18%, recall at 84.01% and F1 score at 79.90%. Reported model level latency is 0.68 ms for voice, 0.01 ms for text, and 0.03 ms for multimodal inference. Additionally, this model is tested on sample data sets for real-life situations to generate prediction of stress levels along with their respective probabilities. This proposal aims at providing an easy-to-use tool to monitor your own stress levels and make decisions instead of a medical diagnosis by you. Therefore, these benchmarks must be considered within their own datasets' properties, divisions between experiments, and requirements for separate empirical testing on actual situations. It is also a part of this system that supports voice and text inputs, prediction histories, statistics summary data analysis tools for health care applications.
