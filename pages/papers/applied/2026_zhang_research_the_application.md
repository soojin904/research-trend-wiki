# Research on the Application of Social Media Big Data Analysis in Cultural Communication

**저자**: Luyuan Zhang
**출처**: International Journal of Knowledge Management, Vol.22, pp.1-15
**발행일**: 2026-09-29
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4018/ijkm.423391

## 초록 (원문)

Against a digital-era backdrop, social media platforms generate large volumes of cultural communication data. Existing scholarship largely concentrates on technical demonstrations yet lacks theory-grounded large-sample empirical analysis. Practical challenges including imprecise audience targeting and inadequate effect evaluation arise from absent multi-dimensional evaluation frameworks. This study conceptualizes cultural communication as a dynamic interaction between communicators and audiences. With the help of Extreme Gradient Boosting-SHapley Additive exPlanations (XGBoost-SHAP), emotional scoring, and social network analysis, this paper studies the multi-platform social media data set of millions. Empirical results identify the gradient-diffusion pattern of cultural hotspots, the quantifiable optimal parameters of short video content, and the stronger cross-circle communication ability of vertical small and medium-sized bloggers. This study constructs a theory-driven analysis process to provide feasible strategies for data-oriented cultural communication.
