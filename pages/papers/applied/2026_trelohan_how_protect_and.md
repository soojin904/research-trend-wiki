## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 대규모 국가 차원의 생물다양성 정책 공청회 의견 서류로부터 시민 여론의 요구점과 격렬하게 대립하는 양극화(Polarization) 쟁점을 AI를 통해 어떻게 분류 정량화할 수 있는가?

**방법론**:
- 프랑스에서 진행된 80,000명의 시민 참여, 6,000개 제안 및 180만 표의 대규모 투표 공청회 데이터베이스 수집
- LLM 임베딩 공간 추출 기반의 토픽 모델링을 적용하여 시민들의 기고문 의미론적 클러스터링 수행
- 수렴도 및 양극화 지수(Polarization index) 공식을 정립하여 쟁점 분석

**주요 결과**:
- 서식지 보존, 환경 교육 등 8대 테마를 성공적으로 분류했으며 교육 및 도시 조경 등은 강력한 시민적 합의(Consensus)를 보인 반면 사냥 규제, 탄소세 부과 등은 극도로 양극화되어 있음을 발견하여 맞춤형 갈등 관리 정책 모델을 도출함


## 초록 (원문)

Despite increasing efforts to promote biodiversity protection, a persistent gap remains between policy measures and public expectations. Aligning biodiversity strategies with citizens' concerns is crucial to fostering support and ensuring effective implementation. Building on the environmental governance and ecosystem services literature, this study leverages Large Language Models for embedding-based topic modeling to analyze a large-scale public consultation involving 80,000 participants, 6000 proposals, and 1.8 million votes in France, identifying eight key biodiversity-related themes: Management of natural and urban spaces, Resource management and pollution control, Wildlife protection and species management, Environmental education and awareness, Agriculture and sustainable food practices, Environmental policy and regulatory measures, Sustainable mobility, energy, and marine conservation, and Emerging and niche concerns. Findings also reveal significant polarization in areas such as hunting regulations, taxation of polluting products, and energy transition, while topics like environmental education and urban greening enjoy strong consensus. These insights highlight the need for differentiated strategies: prioritizing immediate action in consensus areas while fostering dialogue and adaptive policymaking in highly polarized domains. Methodologically, this study demonstrates how Large Language Models can efficiently process and analyze large-scale citizen consultation datasets, offering a scalable approach to capturing public preferences and societal debates in a policy context. • Citizen consultation data reveal contrasting priorities on biodiversity governance. • Regulating services triggers more polarization than cultural or provisioning services. • The polarization index identifies deep divides on hunting, energy, and dietary choices. • LLM-based clustering uncovers public themes from large-scale participatory data. • The scalable method enables thematic and polarization analysis in public consultations.
