## 초록 (원문)

This study examines publication patterns and thematic developments in library and information science (LIS) through book reviews published in College &amp; Research Libraries (1997–2023). Employing Co-LCSH (co-occurrence analysis of Library of Congress Subject Headings) network analysis, the findings reveal an unexpected increase in book review frequency, contrasting with declines in other fields. Additionally, a temporal lag between the emergence of new research themes and their broader representation in LIS trends underscores the reflective role of book reviews.
