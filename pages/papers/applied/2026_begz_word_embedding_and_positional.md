## 초록 (원문)

Positional encodings are techniques used in neural networks to provide information for understanding the structure of sequence in a text and to handle a data more effectively. Positional encodings added to the input embedding’s present effective techniques to develop text extraction models. The integration of word embeddings and positional encodings has vital importance for developing text extraction techniques in Mongolian language.
