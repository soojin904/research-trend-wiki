# Uncovering network changes in the evolution of an innovation niche
**제목(한글)**: 혁신 니치 진화에서 네트워크 변화 발견

**저자**: Edgardo Sica; Pasquale Marcello Falcone; Antonio Lopolito

## 한국어 요약

**연구질문**: 이탈리아 바이오연료 산업의 혁신 니치가 발전하면서 네트워크 구성과 구조는 어떻게 변화하는가?

**방법론**:
- 소셜 네트워크 분석(SNA)을 이탈리아 바이오연료 산업의 4가지 상호 연관 네트워크에 적용
- 각 네트워크의 발전 단계와 성숙 단계 비교 분석
- 밀도, 중심화, 소세계성(small-world-ness) 등 구조적 지표 분석

**주요 결과**:
- 니치 진화 과정에서 행위자들이 새로운 관계를 형성해 밀도가 증가하고 네트워크가 중앙화됨
- 4개 중 2개 네트워크는 소세계 구성을 유지, '커뮤니케이션' 네트워크는 소세계성 증가
- 정책 입안자가 후발 행위자 참여 촉진과 클러스터화를 지원해야 함을 시사

## 초록 (원문)

The present paper aims at unravelling the networking dynamics regarding the evolution of an innovation-niche, by looking at its network configuration over time. To this end, we investigate the actors’ role within the network as well as the network architecture. We employ the social network analysis (SNA) to four different but interrelated types of networks characterising the Italian biofuel industry. Each network was investigated in both its development and maturity phase. Our findings show that, over the niche evolution, actors established new relations, increasing networks’ density and making the networks more centralized. Furthermore, while two out of four networks confirmed their small world configuration over time, one (i.e. “communication” network) increased its small-world-ness, providing a larger number of information channels and more social reinforcement chances for niche actors to innovate. Overall, results suggest that policy makers should accompany and ease the innovation-niche evolution path by fostering the participation of the laggard behind and actors' clusterization, moderating any possible lock-in risk. • Analyzing network dynamics in innovation niche evolution over time. • Niche networks become denser and more centralized over time. • Increased small-world-ness enhances collaboration and knowledge flow. • Supporting lagging actors fosters sustainable niche development.
