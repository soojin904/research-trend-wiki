## 초록

The process to identify the influential customers in a specific network is actually similar to identifying the probability of a customer to make churn or purchase something. This chapter covers a great variety of network metrics in terms of network analysis. These network metrics consider the overall distance and the path among the nodes within the network, the strength of the links connecting the nodes, and the strength or value of the nodes within the network. The chapter summarizes this theory in relation to the measure of power and influence in network analysis. The measure of power is a network metric, or a set of network centralities, that indicates which nodes have higher values within the interconnected structure. The degree centrality is typically one of the first possible network measures that describes a formula for customer influence. The eigenvector centrality measures the importance of a particular node inside the network.
