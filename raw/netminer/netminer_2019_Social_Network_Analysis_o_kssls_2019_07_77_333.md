# Social Network Analysis of Research Trend of Sport for All

**저자**: Kyungsik Kim
**출처**: Journal of Sport and Leisure Studies, Vol.77, pp.333-342
**발행일**: 2019-07-31
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.51979/kssls.2019.07.77.333

## 초록

Purpose: The purpose of this study is to analyze the changes in the mainstream trends of sport for all research and to identify trends in research of sport for all conducted up to now. In this study, research problems were set up as follows: First, what are the trends in the key words of top-level of sport for all? Second, how do trends in the key words of research of sport for all appear?BR Method: The study searched journal papers for DbPia and Korean academic information (kiss) sites to identify research trends in sport for all. Specifically, Focusing on three key words sport for all, life physical education, and life sports, one of these three key words included in the title of the paper were chosen as the subjects. Data analysis was done using NetMiner program technique. Specifically, frequency analysis was conducted to investigate the change of major key words by year, and centrality analysis was conducted to clarify the intellectual structure of sport for all research.BR Result: First, there was no significant change in trends at the top. In other words, sport, satisfaction, leisure, program, life, students, school, exercise, health, etc. paired key words in the top were sport-satisfaction, life-satisfaction, sport-life, leisure-sport, leisure-satisfaction, and so on. Second, the main subjects of sport for all research which had been studied from 2000 to 2018 were sport, satisfaction, leisure, life, student, exercise, school, education, job, health, and stress. The main theme of sport for all research which appeared more than 20 times consisted of 1st group based on exercise, 2nd groups based on sport, 3th groups based on student, and 4th groups based on satisfaction.BR Conclusion: For the past two decades, research of sport for all have not changed much, and it was mainly to verify the effect of sports participation in social psychology.
