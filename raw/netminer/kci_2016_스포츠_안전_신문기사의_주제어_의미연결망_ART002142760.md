# 스포츠 안전 신문기사의 주제어 의미연결망 분석

**저자**: Kim, Kyung-Sik; HYUN BO RAM; 이병국; JangMi Ran

## 초록 (원문)

Pupose: This study was to identify the meaning network structures of sport safety keywords in the newspapers. That is, this study was to analyze the main changes of sport safety keywords in the newspapers according to time and to investigate report trends of sport safety keyword in newspaper that has been reported the last 10 years. Methods: This study collected the keywords of sport safety which has been reported between 2006 and 2015. This study used Big Kinds News Big Data(http:\\www.kinds.or.kr) to search news related sport safety and classified the period into two; 1st period was from 2006 to 2010, 2nd period was from 2011 to 2015. Total number of news are 107 and Total number of keywords were 305. NetMiner 4.2.2 was used to conduct the semantic network analysis. This study performed centrality analysis and power law. Conclusion: The conclusion were as follows: First, there showed important change of newspaper report trend of sport safety keyword. Second, a few keyword contributed to the formation of semantic network structure of sport safety keyword in the newspaper. These keywords with high degree centrality included; (a) safety, (b) South African World Cup, (c) USA, (d) FIFA, (e) safety guard, (f) stadium, and These keyword with high betweenness centrality included; (a) safety, (b) South African World Cup, (c) Brazil, (d) stadium. A small number of hub keywords appeared in the newspapers with many keywords.
