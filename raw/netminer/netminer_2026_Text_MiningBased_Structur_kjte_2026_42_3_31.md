# Text Mining-Based Structural Analysis of Teaching Practicum Experiences: Relationships, Instruction, and Reflection

**저자**: J. H. Lee; Eun-Young Son
**출처**: Korean Journal of Teacher Education, Vol.42 No.3, pp.733-754
**발행일**: 2026-05-30
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.14333/kjte.2026.42.3.31

## 초록

Purpose: This study aimed to systematically identify the experiences, emotions, and underlying meaning structures of pre-service secondary teachers by analyzing their reflective essays on teaching practicum using text mining techniques. Methods: Reflective essays were collected from 180 pre-service teachers who participated in teaching practicums at middle and high schools between 2022 and 2025. Keyword network analysis, including degree and closeness centrality, along with topic modeling, was conducted using NetMiner 4.5, and the topic modeling results were further supplemented and validated using RStudio 4.4.3. Results: The results identified ‘experience’ as the central concept, with key teaching practicum-related keywords such as ‘anxiety’, ‘observation’, and ‘subject’, along with experiential keywords including ‘relationship’, ‘instruction’, ‘planning’, and ‘effort’, which reflect the core processes involved in the practicum.The PFNet analysis revealed that ‘experience’, ‘anxiety’, ‘observation’, ‘relationship’, and ‘concern’ functioned as central concepts within the network.Both degree centrality and closeness centrality analyses indicated that, centered on ‘experience’, various activities and interactions were interconnected, leading to positive emotional experiences, potential growth, and changes in perception. Topic modeling identified four major themes: teaching and learning activities, teaching practicum experiences and career awareness, difficulties in counseling and relationship building, and anxiety related to classroom management and instruction. Conclusion: These research findings suggest that teaching practicums function as an integrative process through which pre-service teachers develop an understanding of the teaching profession and engage in reflective practice.This study contributes to the literature by providing a data-driven analysis of the structural patterns underlying pre-service teachers’ practicum experiences and offers implications for improving teacher education programs, strengthening school–university partnerships, and supporting counseling-related competencies.
