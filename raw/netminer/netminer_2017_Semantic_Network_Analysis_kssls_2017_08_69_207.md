# Semantic Network Analysis of Newspaper Articles Related to Ahn, Minseok Who Dismissed Choi, Soonsil Gate

**저자**: Kyung Sik Kim
**출처**: Journal of Sport and Leisure Studies, Vol.69, pp.207-216
**발행일**: 2017-08-31
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.51979/kssls.2017.08.69.207

## 초록

This study aims to clarify the key words highlighted in the semantic network of newspaper articles related to Ahn, Minseok who dismissed Choi, Soonsil and to reveal the news report frame through subgroup analysis. This study set up the scope of the study and data collection of An Minseok newspaper article related to Choi, Soonsil, which was reported in the national comprehensive daily newspaper for about 6 months(October 24 ~ 2017). In order to search for articles related to Choi, Soonsil-An, Minseok, the study examined the total number of 8 central jurisdictions(Kyung Hyang Newspaper, Kookmin Daily, Tomorrow Newspaper, Munhwa Ilbo, Seoul Newspaper, Hankyoreh and the Korea Daily). The search terms are ‘Choi, Soonsil’ and ‘An, Minseok’ which were reported in 621 articles. In this study, we analyzed data through search and collection of relevant newspaper articles and semantic network analysis. In other words, we searched articles related to Choi, Soonsil and An, Minseok, and organized them into excel and extracted only nouns from the articles through semantic network analysis. Data analysis was conducted using word cloud and semantic network analysis program among NetMiner and analyzed for degree centrality, closeness centrality and betweenness centrality. The conclusions drawn from the above results and discussions are as follows. First, the main key words were Choi, Soonsil, Senator, National Assembly, Hearing, National Assembly, President, An, Minseok, Democratic Party and suspicion. Second, the key words showed similar tendency regardless of the type of centralism, eg. Choi, Soonsil, President, suspicion, hearing, National Assembly, Blue House, etc. There were key words in 6 subgroups respectively. Third, major characters showed a similar tendency regardless of the type of centralism. Park, Geunhye, An, Minseok, Choi, Taemin, Kim, Jong, et cw. ere high in the center of the activity. Park, Geunhye and An, Minseok were also high in the mediation center. There were three subgroups of characters and core characters in each subgroup.
