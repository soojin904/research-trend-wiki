# A Systematic Literature Review on Service Research : Focus on Bibliometrics and Keyword Network Analyses

**저자**: Eui Beom Jeong; Dae Soo Kim
**출처**: Journal of Korea Service Management Society, Vol.19 No.4, pp.267-291
**발행일**: 2018-11-30
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.15706/jksms.2018.19.4.013

## 초록

This study intended to investigate the issues and trends on service research up to date using a systematic literature review based on bibliometrics and keyword network analyses. Searching the Scopus database using author keywords, we extracted 2,032 papers published in premier business journals based on the recent five-year average impact factor. Using NetMiner, a network analysis tool, we analyzed not only author affiliations and co-author relationships but also the entire service research issues and trends by constructing a keyword network. We also examined detailed research topics and emerging trends by periods through component and centrality analyses. The significance of this study lies in providing the overall spectrum of service research up to date through a systematic literature review.
