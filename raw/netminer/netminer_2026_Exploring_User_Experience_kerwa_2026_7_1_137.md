# Exploring User Experience Structures for Activating Taekwondowon: An Application of Text Mining

**저자**: Kyo-Sik Kim; Kyeong-Hoon Park; Yun-Ho Kim
**출처**: Korea Exercise Rehabilitation Welfare Association, Vol.7 No.1, pp.137-151
**발행일**: 2026-03-31
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.66298/kerwa.2026.7.1.137

## 초록

The purpose of this study was to explore the components and structural characteristics of user experiences reflected in visitor reviews of Taekwondowon by applying text-mining techniques and to derive implications for its activation. Online reviews related to Taekwondowon were selected as the analysis data, and a total of 898 reviews were collected and analyzed using term frequency (TF), term frequency–inverse document frequency (TF-IDF), latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA), and NetMiner 4.0. The TF-IDF analysis showed that the keyword “best” had the highest value (114.1), followed by “children,” “performance,” and “taekwondo,” and the comparison of topic proportions indicated that a five-topic model demonstrated the highest coherence score (0.483), leading to the selection of five topics as the optimal structure. The results of keyword degree centrality analysis by topic revealed that Topic 1 was characterized by monorail (0.937), observatory (0.826), and experience (0.569), Topic 2 by taekwondo (0.449), facilities (0.354), and programs (0.263), Topic 3 by taekwondo (1.713), performance (1.369), and children (0.970), Topic 4 by taekwondo (0.635), performance (0.521), and observatory (0.426), and Topic 5 by performance (0.534), monorail (0.459), and taekwondo (0.374). Based on the interpretation of core keywords, Topic 1 was identified as mobility and convenience facilities within the tourist site, Topic 2 as global training programs and environments, Topic 3 as taekwondo performances and experiential tourism, Topic 4 as visitor experience and satisfaction, and Topic 5 as taekwondo demonstrations and natural environments. These findings provide foundational data for establishing future operational directions and activation strategies for Taekwondowon by identifying its structural characteristics and improvement needs from a user-centered perspective.
