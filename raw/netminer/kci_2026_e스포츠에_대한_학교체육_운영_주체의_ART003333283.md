# e스포츠에 대한 학교체육 운영 주체의 인식*

## 초록 (원문)

목적: 이 연구의 목적은 학교체육 운영 주체가 인식하는 e스포츠의 의미 구조를 탐색하고, 학교체육 맥락에서 e스포츠의 교육적 수용 가능성을 분석하는 데 있다.
방법: 학교체육 운영 주체인 ‘학생’과 ‘교사’ 총 788명을 대상으로 온라인 기반 개방형 설문을 실시하여 자료를 수집하였다. 수집된 텍스트 자료는 Textom을 활용하여 표준화 및 정제 과정을 거친 후 키워드 빈도 및TF-IDF 분석을 실시하였다. 키워드 간 의미 구조를 확인하기 위해 공출현 행렬을 구성하고 연결중심성 지수를 산출하였으며, NetMiner 5.0 프로그램을 활용하여 의미연결망을 분석하였다.
결과: e스포츠에 대한 학교체육 운영 주체의 인식은 1) 긍정적 인식: ‘재미’와 ‘스포츠’가 핵심 키워드로 ‘협동심’, ‘전략’, ‘능력’ 등의 키워드와 연결되어, e스포츠를 ‘전략형 스포츠’로 인식하고 있었다. 2) 부정적 인식: ‘중독’이핵심 키워드로 ‘게임’, ‘건강’, ‘시력’, ‘학업’ 등의 키워드와 연결되어, e스포츠를 ‘일상을 위협하는 게임’으로인식하고 있었다. 3) 개선 인식: ‘장비’가 핵심 키워드로 ‘교육’, ‘교사’, ‘학생’, ‘환경’, ‘운영’ 등의 키워드와연결되어, 학교체육 활용을 위해 ‘물리적 환경 구축’과 ‘교육적 운영 체계’가 필요함을 인식하고 있었다.
결론: e스포츠의 교육적 활용은 활동 자체의 특성보다 교육 환경, 운영 체계, 지도 주체와 같은 제도적 조건에 의해 좌우된다. 따라서 e스포츠의 학교체육 수용은 교육적 의미를 어떻게 구조화하고 운영하는가에 따라그 가치가 달라진다. 이는 디지털 전환 시대 학교체육의 포용적 확장을 위한 실천적 기반을 제공할 수 있다.

## Abstract

Purpose: This study aimed to explore the semantic structure of esports as perceived by stakeholders in school physical education and to examine its educational acceptability within the context of school physical education Methods: Data were collected through an online open-ended survey administered to 788 stakeholders in school physical education, including students and teachers. The collected textual data were standardized and refined using Textom, followed by keyword frequency and TF-IDF analyses. To examine the semantic structure among keywords, a co-occurrence matrix was constructed and degree centrality was calculated. In addition, semantic network analysis was conducted using NetMiner 5.0.
Results: The perceptions of stakeholders in school physical education toward esports were classified into three dimensions.
First, in terms of positive perceptions, the keywords fun and sport emerged as central terms and were linked with such keywords as cooperation, strategy, and ability, indicating that esports was perceived as a strategy-based sport. Second, in terms of negative perceptions, the keyword addiction appeared as the central term and was connected with such keywords as game, health, vision, and academic performance, suggesting that esports was perceived as a game that threatens everyday life. Third, in terms of improvement-oriented perceptions, the keyword equipment functioned as the central term and was linked with education, teacher, student, environment, and operation, reflecting the recognition that both physical infrastructure and an educational operating system are necessary for the effective use of esports in school physical education.
Conclusion: The educational use of esports is determined less by the characteristics of the activity itself than by institutional conditions such as the educational environment, operational system, and instructional agents. Accordingly, the value of accepting esports within school physical education depends on how its educational meaning is structured and implemented.
These findings provide a practical foundation for the inclusive expansion of school physical education in the era of digital transformation.
