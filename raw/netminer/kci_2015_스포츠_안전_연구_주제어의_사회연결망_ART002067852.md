# 스포츠 안전 연구 주제어의 사회연결망 분석

**저자**: Kim, Kyung-Sik
**출처**: 한국융합과학회지, Vol.4 No.2, pp.7-21
**발행**: 2015.12

## 초록 (원문)

Pupose: This study was to analyze the network change process to understand the research trend of sport safety and to investigate the degree which keywords contributed to the formation of intellectual structure within sport safety. Methods: This study collected the keywords related sport safety which was published between 1990 and 2015. This study used KSI(Korean Studies Information), Dbpia, RISS to search papers and divided the period into two; 1 period was from 1990 to 2005, 2 period was from 2006 to 2015. Total number of Papers are 76 and Total number of keywords are 276. NetMiner was used to conduct the social network analysis. This study analyzed degree centrality and betweenness centrality, power law. Conclusion: The conclusion were as follows: First, there showed significant change in the research trend of sport safety. Second, a few keywords contributed to the formation of intellectual structure of sport safety. These keywords with high degree centrality included; (a) safety accident, (b) sports, (c) leisure sports, (d) safety management, (e) safety activities, (f) accident, (g) ocean, and These keywords with high betweenness centrality included; (a) safety accident, (b) leisure sports, (c) sports. The keyword network follow the power law, in which a small number of core keywords appeared in the newspapers with many keywords. The systematic studies on sport safety will be needed and discussed in the seminar or congress.
