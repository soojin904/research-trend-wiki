# Analysis of Research Trends Related to Adolescent Depression using Keyword Network Analysis and Topic Modeling

**저자**: Hee-Soon Shin; Moonkyeong Jeong
**출처**: , Vol.11 No.1, pp.117-138
**발행일**: 2026-02-28
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.48033/jss.11.1.6

## 초록

This study aimed to analyze trends in research on adolescent depression published in domestic academic journals using keyword network analysis and topic modeling. To this end, 664 papers published in domestically registered (or candidate) journals were analyzed. NetMiner 4 was used to conduct keyword network analysis and topic modeling. Keyword network analysis included keyword frequency and co-occurrence analysis, as well as centrality analyses such as degree centrality, closeness centrality, and betweenness centrality. Topic modeling analysis identified latent topics and keywords within the papers. Keyword frequency analysis revealed suicide, self-esteem, social support, overprotection, and smartphone overreliance as key keywords. Topic modeling analysis revealed four key topics: Topic 1 (Stress and Media Dependence), Topic 2 (Violence and Social Maladjustment), Topic 3 (Emotional and Psychological Resources), and Topic 4 (Parenting Attitudes and Social Relationships). The results of this study are expected to contribute to suggesting future directions for research on youth depression and providing basic data for establishing related policies.
