## 초록

This thesis analyzes modern pedagogical approaches to developing students’ media competence in the higher education process. In today’s digital society, the increasing volume of information, the widespread use of the Internet and social networks, and the active introduction of digital technologies into education require students to effectively work with media information, critically analyze it, evaluate its reliability, and create independent media products. From this perspective, developing media competence has become one of the important objectives of higher education. The study examines the potential of competency-based, learner-centered, activity-based, constructivist, digital, and project-based learning approaches for developing media competence. Particular attention is also paid to the interdisciplinary integration of media education, the use of interactive methods, involving students in the creation of media products, and the development of critical thinking and information security skills. The integration of these approaches contributes to the development of students’ competencies for independent, critical, creative, and responsible activity in the modern media environment.
