## 초록

This article analyzes the linguistic and sociolinguistic features of lexical units borrowed from English into modern Uzbek. As a result of globalization and the development of digital technologies, education, business, mass media, and social networks, the number of English-origin words in the Uzbek vocabulary and the scope of their use are expanding. The study examines the semantic, phonetic, graphic, morphological, and functional characteristics of these borrowings, as well as the processes through which they adapt to the Uzbek language. It highlights the role of sociolinguistic factors such as age, professional activity, communicative environment, level of education, and the use of the internet and social networks in the use of English loanwords. The analysis demonstrates that English borrowings have become one of the active sources enriching the modern Uzbek lexicon; however, their use is directly influenced by literary language norms, the availability of Uzbek equivalents, and the communicative context.
