# Problematic Internet and social network use. What do we know and what we don't?

**저자**: Elisa Wegmann; Annica Kessling
**출처**: Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research)
**발행일**: 2026-07-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21628929
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Since the 1990s, the problematic use of the internet has been discussed as a potential health issue, whereby the activities that individuals did online then might differ slightly from the activities carried out nowadays. Especially with the upcoming integration of social networks and the popularity of the smartphone, research highlights the importance of understanding the effects of social networks use in daily life. It includes the investigation of positive aspects on well-being such as social support and social integration, but also that the usage of social networks has been related to negative consequences for the individual such as experiences of social exclusion, cyberbullying, fear of missing out, body dysmorphic symptoms as well as addictive usage tendencies. The current chapter provides a short overview regarding the historical development of generalized and specific types of problematic internet use by focusing on problematic social networks use and its positive and negative usage consequences. Therefore, relevant theoretical approaches of problematic internet use in general and more specifically regarding the explanation of the development and maintenance of problematic social networks use will be presented. They postulate the link to substance-use disorder and other addictive behaviors (e.g., gaming and gambling disorders). A proximity is considered to the classification of problematic (internet) use with gaming disorder, classified as disorder due to addictive behavior in the eleventh International Classification System from the World Health Organization. Based on this, terminological recommendations, diagnostic procedures, and comorbidities will be presented. This will be expanded by an empirical overview regarding predisposing variables, affective and cognitive mechanisms, and negative consequences due to the problematic use of the internet and social networks separately, as well as through a comparison of both problematic behaviors. The comparison of the empirical findings emphasizes that besides communalities and divergent mechanisms of different problematic behaviors, social network specific mechanisms seem to play a crucial role in specific disordered usage behaviors. It also allows the formulation of initial considerations regarding potential intervention and treatment approaches for strengthening emotion regulation strategies and self-regulation to reduce online behavior or to define protective mechanisms against a problematic use. The chapter concludes with suggestions as to what should be investigated in future research and what direction should be considered to better understand the specifics of problematic internet use in general, but also the mechanisms involved in the development and maintenance of problematic social network use.
