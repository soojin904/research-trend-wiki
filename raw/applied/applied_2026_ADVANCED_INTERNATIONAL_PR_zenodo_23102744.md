# ADVANCED INTERNATIONAL PRACTICES IN DIAGNOSING AND FORECASTING SOCIAL STRATIFICATION PROCESSES: EVIDENCE FROM THE YEARS 2022–2026 AND IMPLICATIONS FOR UZBEKISTAN

## 초록

This article presents a comparative analysis of international practices in diagnosing and forecasting social stratification processes. The analysis is based on reports of international organisations, official statistical releases and peer-reviewed studies published in the years 2022–2026. It examines the OECD measure of inequality of opportunity, the local-level Social Mobility Index of the United Kingdom, the use of linked administrative records and large-scale social network data in the United States, the microsimulation-based flash estimates of poverty and inequality produced by Eurostat, and the World Bank Poverty and Inequality Platform. The findings show that current practice rests on three interrelated components: comparable measures of opportunity and mobility, frequent and linked microdata, and models that estimate indicators before survey results become available. The existing monitoring base in Uzbekistan, comprising the annual household budget survey used for the official poverty measure and the monthly World Bank survey of citizens, is assessed as a starting point for adopting these instruments, and directions for their application in national practice are proposed.
