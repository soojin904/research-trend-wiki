# Sustainable leadership, interpersonal emotion regulation, job characteristics, and employees’ behavioral outcome in SMEs

**저자**: Qaisar Iqbal
**출처**: Human Resources Management and Services, Vol.8 No.3
**발행일**: 2026-07-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.18282/hrms5987
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

The study aims to examine the quality of leader-member exchange (LMX) as a mediator, as well as leader interpersonal emotion regulation (LIER) and job characteristics (JC) as moderators, in the relationship between sustainable leadership (SL) and employee-related innovative work behaviors (IWB). Using a sample of 207 employees of SMEs in Saudi Arabia, the authors found that SL did not significantly influence IWB through LMX quality. The empirical findings confirm that the direct relationship between SL and LMX quality was significantly dependent on LIER. Moreover, JC did not moderate the relationship between LMX and IWB. This work contributes to the knowledge about the emerging leadership style of SL and the role of emotions in leading people. It is the first to apply the LMX theory in research on SL, and it adds to the understanding of the antecedents of various employee-related outcomes.
