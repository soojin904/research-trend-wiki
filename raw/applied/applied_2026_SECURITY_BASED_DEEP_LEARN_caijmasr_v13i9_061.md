# SECURITY BASED DEEP LEARNING AI ASSISTANT FOR THE INTELLECTUALLY CHALLENGED AGAINST CYBERSECURITY THREATS

## 초록

Digital inclusion is the concept where Information and Communication Technology (ICT) provide easy, reliable, and effective accessibility to all categories of users including the intellectually challenged which are seen as the most vulnerable to the unsecured systems. It was observed that most of the systems were developed without considering the fact that, different categories of users will use them including people living with disabilities. This paper focuses on the security based deep learning AI assistant for the intellectually challenged against cybersecurity threat. This will assist these categories of users against cybersecurity threat. It will empower them with the use of assistive technology to promote digital inclusiveness and equally increase their user experience. Theoretical framework on deep learning assistive AI system and cybersecurity were reviewed and explored. The design and methodology was based on development of security based Deep learning AI assistive model for the intellectually challenged using Department of Special Education in a renowned Federal University in Nigeria. During the process of this research, we discovered that most of the work reviewed did not focus on the concerned security for these categories of users. Also their systems did not go through usability test using standard usability metrics test. From the standard usability metrics test, it was discovered that the system achieved its objectives accurately by controlling the cybersecurity threat with the use of AI based assistive systems. The result of this work showed that there is a digital marginalization of the intellectually challenged as a result of their constant exposure to the cybersecurity attacks.
