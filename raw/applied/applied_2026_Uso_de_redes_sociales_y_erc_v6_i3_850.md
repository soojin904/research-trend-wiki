# Uso de redes sociales y rendimiento escolar en estudiantes de secundaria - 2026

## 초록

The purpose of this research was to determine the relationship between the use of social networks in the academic performance of high school students in an educational institution in Ica, 2026. The study corresponds to the quantitative approach, of a basic type, with a non-experimental design of the correlational descriptive subgroup, where 102 first-grade students participated, the instruments were applied; questionnaire on social networks and records of notes, the questionnaire has the quality criteria. The descriptive results on the use of social networks 32% have a low use; 52% have a medium use and 16% make a high use of social networks; Regarding school performance, 9% of the students are located at the beginning level; 30% are at the process level; 51% have obtained the expected achievement and 10% present an outstanding achievement of school performance. Regarding the level of correlation, it was determined using the Rho Spearman test -0.642; with a significance value of p=0.000. Therefore, there is an moderate inverse correlation between the use of social networks and school performance, that is, the higher the level of use of social networks, the lower the school performance or vice versa.
