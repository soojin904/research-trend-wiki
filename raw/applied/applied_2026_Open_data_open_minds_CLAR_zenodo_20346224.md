## 초록

This is a presentation given by Barbara McGillivray (King's College London) at the CLARIN in University Curricula Workshop, held in Utrecht on 21–22 April 2026. In an age defined by rapid advances in generative AI, the need for transparent, high‑quality, and reusable language data has never been more urgent. This keynote explores how CLARIN can serve not only as a research infrastructure but as a transformative educational platform that helps students and early‑career researchers develop the critical, data‑informed mindset required for responsible research. Drawing on examples from digital humanities teaching, open science practice, and current work in computational linguistics, the talk highlights how CLARIN’s resources, tools, and standards can support both foundational digital literacy and advanced methodological training. It also considers how open data ecosystems can counterbalance the opacity of large-scale AI models, supporting more reflective, explainable, and reproducible approaches to text analysis.
