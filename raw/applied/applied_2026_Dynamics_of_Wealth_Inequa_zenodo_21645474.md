# Dynamics of Wealth Inequality, Misperception, and Wealth Taxation in an Agent-Based Model

## 초록

We develop an agent-based model to study the co-evolution of wealth inequality, biasedperceptions of wealth, and their influence on wealth taxation and redistribution. The modelintegrates random multiplicative wealth dynamics, flat taxation with partial redistribution,and homophily-driven rewiring of social ties. This combination yields a minimal and trans-parent setting in which the social origins of inequality misperception can be examined in adynamic environment.The model reproduces several stylized facts, including persistent wealth inequality, anegative link between social mobility and wealth concentration, and middle-class perceptionbiases. Crucially, it shows that even under constant wealth change, homophilic rewiringof social networks generate persistent and systematic biases in how individuals perceivetheir relative position and the average wealth in society. These biases create a feedbackloop that reinforces inequality by preventing the emergence of political majorities for moreredistribution.
