# Dichotomous effects of Korean drama: commodification of soft power

**저자**: Preetha Menon; Josraj Arakkal; Damini Goyal Gupta

## 초록

Introduction Hallyu, a term used to refer to the spread of Korean culture, is gaining feverish proportions. The purpose of this study is to analyze the effects of Hallyu, specifically in the context of Korean dramas and their impact on the Korean beauty industry. Methods This paper engages in two studies to collect real-time evidence. The first study was done through netnography of online forums on Instagram, Youtube and Reddit. For the second study we conducted in-depth interviews with viewers of Korean dramas. Results Our findings are arranged in line with the theory of consumption value (TCV). Apart from the five main themes of the TCV, this study contributes a fresh conceptual framework to decipher additional values that the consumers perceive. The sixth theme of trust and credibility value represents a core value which is also a unique contribution of this study. Discussion Through these new findings, this study extends TCV with further inputs. Researchers, content creators and managers in the Korean beauty industry will all benefit from the output of this research.
