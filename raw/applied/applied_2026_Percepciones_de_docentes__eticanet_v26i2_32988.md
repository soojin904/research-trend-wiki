# Percepciones de docentes andaluces sobre el uso de redes sociales en educación

**저자**: Óscar Costa-Román; Aurora Forteza Martínez

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Social networks are a very popular means of communication, and education is a field where they are very popular. The aim is to find out the opinion of Andalusian teachers of Compulsory Secondary Education and Baccalaureate on social networks and their link with education. A quantitative methodology was used through a questionnaire in which a total of 459 individuals participated. Among the main results, teachers believe that social networks can be a good resource to be used in the classroom, although the number of educators who use them in the classroom is low. In conclusion, it should be noted that teachers are not of the opinion that social networks can have more advantages than disadvantages in the classroom, and that they do not consider them to be a relevant element that helps students to consolidate the content taught in the classroom.
