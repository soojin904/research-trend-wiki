# Czasobieda jako problem społeczny w Polsce

## 초록

The belief that work has crucial meaning is deeply rooted in Polish society. This is so strong that work becomes not only a source of income, but also a way of spending free time. Moreover, losing it is associated with such strong psychological experiences that it often leads to suicide attempts. The above results in negative consequences for a part of society who resign from employment due to age. People for whom work was their whole life are confronted with a new reality in which they are forced to look for another way to use their energy and time. In Poland, the problem of time poverty has not been discussed so far. The aim of the article is to show that the mentality according to which work is a basic value for a person is a social problem that requires actions aimed at its elimination and to explain why this is so. The article uses an analysis of available source data and literature.
