# The role of self-efficacy and future goal orientation in enhancing student academic engagement in the digital era

## 초록

This investigation examines the critical psychological intersection of Self-Efficacy (SE) and Future Goal Orientation (FGO) in promoting Academic Engagement (AE) among higher education students, particularly those navigating the high-autonomy landscape of the digital era. Utilizing a rigorous qualitative research design and thematic analysis applied to the rich, narrative responses of 15 tertiary education students, this study aimed to uncover the specific, lived mechanisms that govern motivation, resilience, and long-term aspirations. The central finding indicates that SE functions as a crucial mediator, bridging the gap between abstract, long-term FGO and concrete, daily behavioral AE. Notably, the data chronicles a motivational shift in FGO, moving away from an initial extrinsic drive often focused on honoring family sacrifices, to a deeply intrinsic purpose centered on generating positive social impact and forging a lasting legacy. This powerful internal compass, fortified by high SE (evident in cognitive resilience, tenacious effort, and the constructive interpretation of setbacks), directly manifests as disciplined self-regulation, enabling stringent time management and the successful integration of demanding work and study commitments. Such self-regulatory competencies are the defining features of effective AE in today's flexible learning environments, reinforcing the profound need to nurture robust internal psychological capital for sustainable student success in technologically mediated contexts.
