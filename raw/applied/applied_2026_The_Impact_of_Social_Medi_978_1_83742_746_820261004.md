## 초록

In recent years, social networking has fundamentally altered the landscape of memorabilia collection, particularly in the sports industry. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube have transformed into dynamic hubs where collectors, fans, and influencers converge, reshaping traditional collection methods and market dynamics. This study looks at how social media has changed the way people collect sports memorabilia between 2014 and 2025. With sites such as Instagram, Twitter (X), and Reddit serving as important hubs for fans and collectors, the study examines how digital engagement, influencer culture, and community interaction affect collector behaviour, perceived value, and market dynamics. Data were collected using hashtags such as #SportsMemorabilia, #SportsCollectibles, #SignedJerseys, and #SportsNFTs and then analysed thematically to uncover important patterns in engagement and sentiment. This study explains the connection between community-driven validation, digital trust, and emotional fan identity. It focuses on how live auctions, virtual showcases, and real-time information promote collectors’ social capital and emotional attachment in addition to transactional conduct. By placing memorabilia gathering into the broader digital world, this study contributes to the literature on marketing and digital consumer behaviour. Additionally, it has practical implications for marketers trying to connect with digitally native fan audiences, sports organisations, and collector goods. The study also considers the rise of blockchain-backed collectables such as non-fungible tokens (NFTs), digital oversaturation, and authenticity concerns. Limitations include platform-based data scope and a dataset that is mostly in English. Future research could investigate artificial intelligence (AI)-driven value, cross-cultural collector motives, and the environmental impact of digital artefacts.
