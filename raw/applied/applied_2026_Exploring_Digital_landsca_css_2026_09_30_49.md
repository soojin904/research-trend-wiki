# Exploring Digital landscape, Awareness and Vulnerabilities: A Case Study of Students of Central University of Rajasthan, India

**저자**: Sarath MT; Suman Rana; Wairokpam Premi Devi
**출처**: Contemporary Social Sciences, Vol.35 No.3, pp.49-61
**발행일**: 2026-09-27

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

The objective of this research paper is to analyze the awareness of digital literacy and online security among the students at Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) in relation to the wide gulf in their extensive digital usage versus their insecurity online.A qualitative methodology including two instruments structured questionnaires (n=27) and semi-structured interviews (n=15) were used to analyze student usage and their attitudes toward privacy and security.According to the findings, the students are extremely digitally literate, and their usage hours is around over six hours a day while a very high level of ignorance about online security awareness is noticeable.All 42 participants stated they were unaware of the digital literacy resources being used in the university, demonstrating a clear gap in the institution's outreach, thus, magnifying their insecurity over the internet.Based on the findings, a key recommendation would be the need for implementing mandatory digital literacy and internet security modules across the curriculum along with effective communication on available resources.
