# The Trust-Action Gap: How Digital Trust Mediates the Impact of Influencers, AI, and Product Reviews on Gen Z Purchase Decisions

**저자**: Md Raihanul Islam
**출처**: European journal of management, economics and business., Vol.3 No.1, pp.71-84
**발행일**: 2025-12-30
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.59324/ejmeb.2026.3(1).06
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study investigates the dynamics of the Trust-Action Gap, the discrepancy between high digital trust and finalized purchase behavior within the Generation Z consumer cohort. Using a quantitative, cross-sectional design (N=200), the research employs Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) with bootstrapping to test the mediating role of Digital Trust (TP) in the relationship between three dominant digital information sources: Influencer Marketing (IM), Product Reviews (PR), and AI Recommendations (AI) and Gen Z Purchase Decisions (PD). The empirical results confirm that Digital Trust is a critical, fully mediating variable through which the digital sources exert their influence on purchasing behavior. Specifically, all three antecedents significantly and positively impact Digital Trust (IM → TP, β = 0.35; PR → TP, β = 0.28; AI → TP, β = 0.15), which, in turn, is the strongest predictor of Purchase Decisions ($\beta=0.55$). The mediation analysis reveals a hierarchy of influence, with the relational credibility offered by Influencer Marketing having the largest indirect effect (Effect = 0.193). Crucially, while digital sources successfully cultivate trust, the persistence of unexplained variance in the final Purchase Decision highlights the Trust-Action Gap. This gap is interpreted as a function of Generation Z's inherent economic prudence and rational, post-trust assessment of transactional barriers, such as price, value, and seamless user experience. The findings underscore that for brands targeting this digitally native demographic, trust serves as the necessary psychological gatekeeper, demanding authenticity and transparency (e.g., candid influencer disclosures and Explainable AI). Closing the conversion gap, however, requires strategic intervention focused on lowering economic friction and optimizing the digital transaction experience.
