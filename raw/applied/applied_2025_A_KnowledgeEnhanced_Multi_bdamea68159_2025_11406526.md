# A Knowledge-Enhanced Multi-Task Framework for Intelligent Legal Text Analysis

## 초록

In response to the challenges posed by the continuously expanding volume of judicial documents and the complex structure of legal texts, this paper proposes a knowledge-enhanced legal intelligence framework that integrates pretrained models, legal knowledge graphs, and a prompt-based guidance mechanism. The method leverages both the semantic representations of legal texts and structured legal knowledge, and employs multi-task learning to jointly model charge prediction, statutory recommendation, and sentencing prediction. Experiments conducted on the CAIL2018 dataset and a self-constructed dataset demonstrate that the proposed model significantly outperforms traditional deep learning models and mainstream pretrained models in metrics such as Macro-F1 and MAE. Ablation studies further verify the effectiveness of the knowledge graph and prompts in fact-element extraction, statute matching, and legal-logic modeling. The results indicate that the framework can provide high-precision and scalable technological support for intelligent judicial applications.
