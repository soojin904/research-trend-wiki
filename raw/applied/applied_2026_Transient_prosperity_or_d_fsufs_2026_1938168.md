# Transient prosperity or durable resilience: how place-based specialty agricultural policy shapes rural household welfare in China

**저자**: Zhaohua Zhang; Zhiyou Shi; Shengli Shi
**출처**: Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, Vol.10
**발행일**: 2026-10-02
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.3389/fsufs.2026.1938168

## 초록

Background Improving rural household welfare is central to global sustainable development. China’s place-based Specialty Agricultural Product Advantageous Zones (SAZ) policy promotes specialized agricultural clusters. Nevertheless, existing literature mostly evaluates policy effects based on monetary income, with limited evidence on whether such policy merely delivers short-run transient prosperity or builds long-run durable household development resilience. This study fills this gap by disentangling the policy’s dual welfare outcomes and underlying factor‑reallocation mechanisms Methods Drawing on 2013‑2021 matched panel data combining the China Household Finance Survey (CHFS) micro‑household data and county-level socioeconomic statistics, this paper adopts a multi-period difference‑in‑differences (DID) framework to estimate SAZ impacts on two welfare metrics: multidimensional household income quality (capturing transient prosperity) and model-derived household development resilience (measuring durable resilience). Mediation analysis is further applied to test three transmission pathways: agricultural land intensification, employment structure transformation, and production technology upgrading. Parallel trend tests, placebo permutation, heterogeneous treatment effect diagnostics, threshold changing robustness checks, and instrumental‑variable estimation are implemented to establish causal validity. Results SAZ construction improves household income quality in the short run to generate transient prosperity, and enhances long-term development resilience to build durable resilience. This is mainly attributed to three complementary transmission channels: agricultural land intensification, employment structure transformation, and production technology upgrading. Among them, employment structure transformation serves as the dominant pathway, particularly for long-term development resilience, while production technology upgrading and agricultural land intensification play reinforcing roles. Heterogeneity analysis shows that households in counties with stronger agricultural foundations obtain larger welfare gains, and this advantage is especially pronounced for durable resilience. Across SAZ product categories, all five types significantly raise short-term income quality, but the gains in development resilience are insignificant for aquaculture zones. Across household types, transient prosperity gains are broadly comparable regardless of income structure, while durable resilience gains are concentrated among agriculture-dominated households. Conclusion Place-based specialty-agricultural policy can achieve not only immediate income‑driven prosperity but also durable livelihood resilience via land, labor, and technology factor reallocation. To maximize policy returns, local governments should reform SAZ performance appraisal to incorporate resilience-related indicators, prioritize employment structure upgrading, and implement differentiated support for lagging counties, specific SAZ product categories, and heterogeneous household groups. The findings offer replicable practical references for developing economies pursuing balanced short-run welfare improvement and long-term resilient rural livelihoods.
