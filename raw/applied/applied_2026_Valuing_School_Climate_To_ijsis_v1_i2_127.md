# Valuing School Climate Towards Strengthened Partnership Commitment

**저자**: Ruel G. Agudo
**출처**: International Journal of Sustainable and Integrated Studies, Vol.1 No.2, pp.675-687
**발행일**: 2026-09-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.65339/ijsis.v1.i2.127
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study examined school climate and stakeholder participation toward strengthening school-community partnership commitment in Campao Oriental High School and Pandanon High School, Getafe I District, Bohol. Anchored in Ecological Systems Theory, School Climate Theory, Epstein’s Theory of Overlapping Spheres of Influence, Stakeholder Theory, and Social Capital Theory, it employed a descriptive-correlational design involving 42 respondents comprising 30 teachers and 12 external stakeholders. Data were collected using a structured survey questionnaire incorporating the Organizational Health Inventory and measures of stakeholder participation, and were analyzed using frequency, percentage, weighted mean, standard deviation, ranking, and Kendall’s Tau-b. The overall school climate was Very High (M = 3.6493, SD = 0.29259), with collegial leadership obtaining the highest rating, while overall stakeholder participation was High (M = 3.1933, SD = 0.62816). A strong positive and statistically significant relationship was found between school climate and stakeholder participation (τb = 0.867, p = 0.015). Major challenges included distance and transportation difficulties, conflicting schedules, limited parent and community participation, and inadequate financial and material support. The study concludes that a positive, inclusive, and collaborative school environment is associated with greater stakeholder participation and strengthened school-community partnership commitment. Accordingly, implementation of the School-Community Partnership Commitment Plan was recommended. The study aligns with SDG 4 (Quality Education) by supporting positive and collaborative educational environments and SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals) by strengthening sustained school-community collaboration. Its sustainability impact centers on educational, community, institutional, and governance sustainability through stakeholder engagement, shared responsibility, participatory governance, and sustained collaboration for continuous school improvement.
