## 초록

Psychological health of the people is highly essential to achieve their desired tasks. While the practice of forgiveness enables individuals to focus on inner peace, mindfulness, quality of life, and satisfaction with life. This chapter, therefore, assesses how negatively reinforced feelings can affect physiological wellbeing and health of youth? It is based on previous studies which explored the association of forgiveness with psychological well-being of youths. It highlights the impact of social, economic, environmental, biological, lifestyle and technological fields on psychological health. It discovers the implications of forgiveness on psychological health of youths. Social support, social networks and environmental quality have a positive implication on psychological health. Excessive technological addiction, inappropriate diet patterns and biological characteristics have a negative impact on psychological health. Forgiveness practice has a positive impact on the learning process, psychological wellbeing, social wellbeing and psychological health of people.
