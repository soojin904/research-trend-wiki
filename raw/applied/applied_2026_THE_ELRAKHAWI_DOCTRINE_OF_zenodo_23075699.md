## 초록

THE EL-RAKHAWI DOCTRINE OF COMPREHENSIVE DIGITAL TRUST by Dr. Mohamed Kamal Arafa El-Rakhawi presents a revolutionary unified framework for security, privacy, and policy management across all digital environments. This monumental work redefines trust from a philosophical concept into a mathematically quantifiable engineering parameter, addressing the fundamental challenge of the post-centralized era where traditional perimeter-based security has become obsolete. The doctrine introduces groundbreaking innovations: the El-Rakhawi Trust Quantification Model for dynamic trust evaluation, the Agent Delegation Equation for autonomous systems, the Trust Composition Algebra based on semiring theory, the Cost-of-Corruption Framework for economic security analysis, and the Unified Risk-Trust Security Equation for adaptive security posture computation. These mathematical foundations enable continuous, context-aware trust assessment across ten critical domains: agent-based systems, distributed computing (cloud, peer-to-peer, cluster, grid), ubiquitous IoT environments, decentralized architectures and blockchain, social networks and Web 2.0, wireless and sensor networks, e-learning and virtual organizations, and risk-adaptive security frameworks. The work culminates in the El-Rakhawi Global Digital Trust Governance Framework and the proposed International Digital Trust Treaty (EIDTT), establishing legal and institutional foundations for worldwide digital trust governance. With comprehensive coverage spanning cryptographic protocols, reputation systems, zero-knowledge attestation, post-quantum security, and cross-domain trust federation, this doctrine provides the definitive blueprint for building a trustworthy digital civilization where entities interact across boundaries with mathematical certainty and ethical accountability.
