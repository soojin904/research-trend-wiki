# Jogos online e procrastinação: uma análise sobre o comportamento de brasileiros

**저자**: Richard Silva Veiga; Bruna Godinho Corrêa; Jéssica Puchalski Trettim

## 초록

INTRODUCTION: The prevalence of online games in the daily lives of young people and adults can influence behaviors such as procrastination, which is characterized by the postponement of important tasks and can negatively impact academic, professional, and personal life. OBJECTIVE: To investigate the relationship between procrastination and online gaming, with a particular focus on how time spent playing, players' age, and gaming modality influence the level of procrastination among gamers. METHODS: A cross-sectional study with data collected online via Google Forms from active gaming community members. RESULTS: Procrastination was significantly associated with longer time spent on online games (p=0,001), as well as a preference for team play (p=0,031). Additionally, participants’ age influenced procrastination levels, with younger individuals (18 to 23 years old) presenting higher procrastination averages compared to older age groups (p=0,020). CONCLUSION: In summary, procrastination was found to be associated with time spent playing online games, gaming modality, and players' age. Therefore, although online games may provide leisure and social interaction, excessive use over prolonged periods may be associated with difficulties in behavioral self-regulation, thereby contributing to procrastination.
