## 초록

The monograph Resilient and transforming Europe addresses the issues of Europe’s resilience and transformation in the context of overlapping crises. It is the result of the Jean Monnet Centre of Excellence TRANSFORM EU project, implemented at the Poznań University of Economics and Business. This monograph is a valuable resource for researchers, analysts and practitioners in the ﬁelds of European integration, international economics, economic relations, public policy and business.
