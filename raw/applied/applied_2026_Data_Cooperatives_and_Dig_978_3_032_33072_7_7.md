## 초록

Data cooperatives have been introduced in the EU within the Data Governance Act (DGA, EU Regulation n. 868/2022) as providers of data intermediation services “aimed at establishing commercial relationships for the purpose of sharing data between an indeterminate number of data subjects and data controllers, on the one hand, and data users, on the other, through technical, legal, or other means […].” This is both an innovative and incomplete regulation, which therefore requires, from a legal perspective, a systematic framework for the situation in light of the multiple regulatory frameworks involved. This refers in particular to the Third Sector (Third Sector Code), the protection of personal data (General Data Protection Regulation or GDPR), and the new rules for online intermediaries (Digital Services Act [DSA]).
