# The current state and development prospects of youth sports in Ukraine (finding of a sociological survey)

## 초록

The current functioning of youth and children’s sports organizations in Ukraine takes place under significant organizational, financial, and institutional challenges, intensified by unstable funding, limited resources, and uneven management practices. Despite the strategic importance of youth sports for the formation of a sports reserve and the development of human capital, comprehensive empirical studies assessing the condition of youth sports organizations remain limited. The purpose of this study is to identify key problems, needs, and development prospects of youth sports organizations in Ukraine based on an expert survey of representatives of the sports sector. The study is based on the results of an online survey involving 117 respondents, including coaches, managers of youth sports schools, administrators of sports federations, university lecturers, athletes, and other professionals related to youth sports. Respondents were classified according to age, gender, length of professional experience, position, and field of activity. The questionnaire consisted of both open-ended and closed-ended questions and covered key aspects of the functioning of youth sports organizations, including financial support, personnel policy, motivation of children and parents, quality of sports reserve training, perception of reforms, and the role of public and private sectors in sports development. The findings indicate a complex and heterogeneous state of the sector. Most respondents emphasize insufficient funding, low motivation of coaching staff and parents, lack of systematic modernization, and the need to update management approaches. Significant differences in responses were identified depending on respondents’ age, professional experience, and position: younger specialists tend to support innovative changes, whereas managers focus primarily on legal and financial constraints. The results highlight the necessity of modernizing the management system of youth sports, strengthening cross-sector partnerships, increasing financial sustainability, and creating conditions for the professional development of coaches, as well as for effective motivation models for children and their parents. The study provides a scientific basis for developing practical and policy-oriented recommendations aimed at improving youth sports development in Ukraine.
