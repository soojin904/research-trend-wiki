# STRATEGIC DIPLOMACY OF ANGOLA IN AN INTERNATIONAL SYSTEM IN TRANSFORMATION: THE COUNTRY'S ROLE IN CONFLICT MEDIATION, THE LOBITO CORRIDOR, AND THE GEOPOLITICAL RECONFIGURATION OF AFRICA

## 초록

The accelerated transformation of the international system has produced new disputes of influence, competition for strategic resources, reconfiguration of geopolitical alliances and growing appreciation of the ability of African states to define their own agendas. In this context, Angola assumes a particularly relevant position on the African continent, combining geostrategic location, natural resources, access to the Atlantic, active participation in African peace and security mechanisms and growing importance of the Lobito Corridor. This article analyses the evolution of Angolan strategic diplomacy and seeks to understand how Angola can convert its political, economic, geostrategic and diplomatic capacity into greater international influence. The research adopts a qualitative approach, of exploratory and analytical-descriptive nature, based on a bibliographic and documentary review. It is argued that Angolan diplomacy must evolve from a predominantly political-state diplomacy to a multidimensional strategic diplomacy, capable of articulating conflict mediation, economic diplomacy, regional security, critical mineral resources, connectivity, public diplomacy and relations with the main global powers. The article proposes, as a result, a conceptual model called the Model of Multidimensional Strategic Diplomacy of Angola (DEMA), structured in five dimensions: peace and security; economic diplomacy; geopolitics and connectivity; public diplomacy; and science, technology and innovation. It is concluded that the consolidation of Angola as a regional diplomatic power will depend less on the formal dimension of its external presence and more on the ability to transform geopolitical assets into permanent instruments of influence, negotiation and defense of national interest.
