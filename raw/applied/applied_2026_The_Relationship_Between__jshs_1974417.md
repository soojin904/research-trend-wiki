# The Relationship Between Depression and Anxiety of Loneliness in the Elderly After the Earthquake

## 초록

Aim: This study was conducted to reveal the relationship between loneliness felt by older people after an earthquake and depression and anxiety.Method: This cross-sectional study involved face-to-face interviews with 167 elderly individuals living in Malatya Container City, who were administered the Elderly Loneliness, Geriatric Depression, and Geriatric Anxiety Scales. In the analyses, ANOVA, the Kruskal-Wallis test, Pearson correlation, and hierarchicalregression were used.Results: When examining participants’ anxiety levels, the average total anxiety scale score was 23.87±10.56; among the sub-dimensions, the highest average was observed in the somatic anxiety sub-dimension. When the participants’ depression levels were examined, 33.5% were found to have mild depression, 20.4% had moderate depression, and 10.8% had severe depression. It was found that 16.3% ofthe participants were very lonely, and 4.2% experienced intense loneliness. However, participants with poor economic conditions, those living with relatives, or those living alone were found to experience more symptoms of loneliness, anxiety, and depression.
