## 초록

Recent advancements in location-based technologies have created significant opportunities within the mobile and digital ecosystem.The widespread adoption of geospatial services has transformed the way users interact with mobile applications, enabling personalized recommendations, real-time navigation, location-aware notifications, and contextsensitive services.Modern applications increasingly utilize location intelligence to enhance user engagement and improve service delivery.Rather than relying solely on traditional check-in mechanisms, contemporary systems leverage real-time geographic data to provide smarter and more interactive experiences.Furthermore, location-based services have enabled businesses to connect effectively with users by offering targeted and relevant information based on their current position.The proposed system builds upon these developments by providing an intelligent solution for groups of users, helping them identify an optimal common location based on synchronized geographic coordinates.By analyzing multiple user locations simultaneously, the system facilitates efficient group coordination, reduces travel inconvenience, and improves decision-making for meetings, events, and collaborative activities.
