## 초록

This study examines how social resources are mobilised for sustainable tourism development in Na Hang, Tuyen Quang, Vietnam. Using a qualitative case study approach, the research draws on 16 semi-structured interviews conducted between July and August 2026 with community representatives, state management representatives, tourism businesses and experts. Interview notes were analysed to identify recurring themes related to resource mobilisation, stakeholder coordination, community participation and sustainability. The findings identify four interconnected forms of social resources: local knowledge and capabilities, social networks, organisational and coordination capacity, and institutional connections. Their potential contribution to tourism development depends on access to opportunities, coordination among stakeholders, clear responsibilities and sustained cooperation. The study also distinguishes participation in tourism activities from influence over decision-making. By connecting social resources with mobilisation mechanisms and contextual conditions, it offers an analytical perspective on sustainable tourism development without claiming causal relationships or directly measured sustainability outcomes. The findings provide practical insights for strengthening coordination and community involvement in Na Hang
