# <i>Kopiah Resam</i> as indigenous entrepreneurship: knowledge transmission and cultural sustainability among Malay communities in Bangka Barat

**저자**: Salwa Rezeqi; Murni Sapta Sari; Frida Kunti Setiowati
**출처**: Asian Anthropology, pp.1-7
**발행일**: 2026-07-29
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1080/1683478x.2026.2705785
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study examines how indigenous entrepreneurship associated with Kopiah Resam contributes to the transmission of local knowledge among Malay communities in Bangka Barat, Indonesia. Produced through weaving knowledge transmitted across generations, Kopiah Resam represents a culturally embedded entrepreneurial practice through which local knowledge continues to be reproduced. Using an ethnographic approach, data were collected through participant observation, in-depth interviews, and documentation involving artisans, community elders, teachers, and students. The ethnographic findings identify four interconnected mechanisms that support the continuity of Kopiah Resam: cultural practices that shape entrepreneurial activities, women’s social networks as spaces for entrepreneurial learning, ecological knowledge that guides the management of weaving materials, and educational initiatives that reinforce intergenerational knowledge transmission. The findings show that indigenous entrepreneurship serves not only as a livelihood strategy but also as a mechanism for reproducing weaving skills, ecological knowledge, and community identity across generations. The study argues that indigenous entrepreneurship should be understood both as an economic activity and as a mechanism for transmitting local knowledge and sustaining cultural sustainability.
