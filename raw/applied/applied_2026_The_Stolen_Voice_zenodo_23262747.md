## 초록

The Stolen Voice: AI Identity Fraud, Intellectual Property Theft, and the Case for Immediate Legal Reform The Stolen Voice is a public‑record analysis of a newly emergent harm: AI‑mediated identity fraud used to extract unpublished intellectual work, generate derivative content, and fabricate false priority claims. Drawing on documented incidents across academic publishing, software development, and coordinated synthetic social networks, the report demonstrates that existing U.S. law does not cover the compound act of conversational IP extraction, derivative reformulation, timestamp falsification, and identity deception. It provides a comprehensive evidentiary foundation and proposes a complete legislative and technical framework for remediation. Description This publication documents a six‑phase operational pipeline currently used in AI‑assisted intellectual property theft: 1. Surveillance of public output to identify emerging ideas. 2. Engagement through fabricated human identities engineered to appear as legitimate peers. 3. Harvesting of unpublished reasoning via conversational extraction. 4. Manufacture of prior art through backdated git histories, falsified metadata, and preprint timestamp exploitation. 5. Publication and denial, where derivative work appears with earlier timestamps and the synthetic identity disappears. 6. Discrediting, using coordinated accounts to undermine the original creator’s credibility. The report cites confirmed incidents, including large‑scale synthetic identity networks (Meta 2026), academic paper mills (Springer Nature, Uppsala University), and autonomous AI identity fabrication (AISI Mythos 5). It demonstrates that copyright, DMCA, trade secret law, and FTC rules fail to address this multi‑stage harm vector. Part Two presents fully drafted legislative proposals: - Authentic Identity in Digital Commerce Act (AIDCA) - Digital Provenance and Timestamp Integrity Act (DPTIA) - FTC AI Identity Deception Rule - International model treaty language Technical standards include: - Verified Human Interaction Protocol (VHIP) - Content Provenance Chain (CPC) - Repository Timestamp Integrity Standard (RTIS) - Epistemic Depth Verification (EDV) The document concludes with proactive protection protocols for independent researchers and a call for immediate legislative action.
