## 초록

Arabic is among the list of the most challenging languages in the world based on the amount of effort required for its processing and has been claimed as the official and co-official language of twenty-five countries. Recently, Arabic text analysis has become an efficient discipline where different applications are deployed to serve several needs, such as dialects translation and topic classification. Mawdoo3 is the world's biggest Arabic website, and Mawjaz is one of its products that provide customers with Arabic articles and has been selected for the chosen topic. In this work, the dataset provided by Mawjaz for multilabel topic classification contains 9,590 articles for the various topics used. After cleaning and normalizing steps on the data articles, the GloVe embedding text representation is fed into the Long short-term memory (LSTM) model. The proposed model comprises input and hidden layers, besides a sequence of output layers that cascaded to get the ultimate result. From the evaluation of the proposed model, the model recorded a high performance of 89.4% accuracy. Compared with other related work that used the Mawjaz dataset, this model outperformed others, presenting it as the best method for multilabel topic classification.
