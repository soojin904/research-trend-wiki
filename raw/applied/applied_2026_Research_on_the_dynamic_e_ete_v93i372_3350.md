# Research on the dynamic evolution and influencing factors of structural power in the value chain of China’s service industry

## 초록

The structural power index of China’s service industry value chain has a significant gap with developed countries and service giants such as the United States. The overall position of such value chain’s structural power still relies mainly on its contribution as a value-added input country, while as a value-added export country, the ability of the world economy to form import dependence on China’s service industry intermediate goods still needs to be improved. The growth of this country’s digital trade volume and the improvement of the quality of the global digital trade network are conducive to the upgrading of the structural power of China’s service industry value chain. Among them, the density of the digital trade network in the European Union region has the most significant impact, followed by the “the Belt and Road” region, and the impact of North America has not yet emerged.
