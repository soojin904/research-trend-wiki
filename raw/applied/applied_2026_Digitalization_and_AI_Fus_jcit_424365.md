# Digitalization and AI Fusion of Multilingual Language Services in Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area

**저자**: Yuxi Zhang; Xinling Wu; Mingyu Wang; Shengnu Chi
**출처**: Journal of Cases on Information Technology, Vol.28 No.1, pp.1-21

## 초록

Aiming at the multilingual service in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, this study constructs a full-link-oriented digital language service ecological model, innovatively proposes adaptive knowledge distillation and a cross-domain corpus intelligent cleaning algorithm, and realizes the closed-loop service by integrating a multilingual artificial intelligence hub and a unified application programming interface gateway. Experiments show that the average delay of this scheme is only 318 milliseconds under the high concurrency of 200 queries per second, the noise rate of the corpus is reduced from 18.6% to 4.2%, and the editing distance after translation is shortened by 11%–15%, which effectively solves the performance gap between data islands and long-tailed languages. This study provides an ecological solution with both technical feasibility and institutional foresight for the digital transformation of regional language services.
