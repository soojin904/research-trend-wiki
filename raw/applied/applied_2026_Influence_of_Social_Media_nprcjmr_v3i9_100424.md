# Influence of Social Media Marketing on Generation Z Purchasing Behavior: The Mediating Role of Word of Mouth

## 초록

Background: Social media networking has become an increasingly important tool for influencing consumer behavior, especially among Gen Z, who are highly engaged with social media networks. In Nepal, limited research has examined how various dimensions of social media network marketing influence purchasing behaviors. This study seeks to examine the influence of entertainment, customization, interaction, and trendiness on purchasing behavior among Gen Z, with word of mouth as a mediating variable. Methods: The study employed an explanatory research design with data collected from 488 Gen Z consumers in Kathmandu Valley. The data collected were analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS SEM) to examine the relationships between dimensions of social network marketing, Word of mouth, and Purchasing behavior. Findings: Findings revealed that all four dimensions of social network marketing i.e., entertainment, customization, interaction, and trendiness, had a significant effect on purchasing behavior of Gen Z. Trendiness demonstrated the strongest effect among all indicating youth are influenced by current, fashionable and up to date social media content. WOM significantly mediated all four dimensions of social media marketing, demonstrating that Gen Z communicates and share information with others. Thus, marketers should develop timely, personalized, engaging, interactive, and shareable social media platforms to effectively influence young generations. Conclusions: Social Media Marketing plays a significant role in influencing young generations' purchasing behavior in Nepal, with trendiness as an important dimension and WOM as an important mediating variable. The study extends Glasser’s Choice Theory to explain Gen Z purchasing behavior. The findings have practical implications for policymakers and marketers empathizing the need for digital marketing. Novelty: The study provides a context-specific framework linking social media marketing dimensions, word of mouth, and Gen Z purchasing behavior, offering new empirical evidence from Nepal’s social commerce market.
