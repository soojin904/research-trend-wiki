# Displacement and the Reshaping of Indigenous Livelihoods: A Review of the Literature on Mumbai’s Koli and Agri Communities

## 초록

The Koli and Agri communities have occupied a visibly glaring space in the landscape of Mumbai since time immemorial.They have made their presence felt not only demographically, but also culturally and politically.However, despite being the city's age-old inhabitants and landowners, both communities have been pushed to the margins for various reasons.From facing a serious bout of identity crisis to being introduced to other religions, the Koli and Agri communities are plagued with a whole host of problems.As a permanent solution to these problems and also for a sense of security, the two communities have chosen to assimilate and subsequently integrate into the mainstream religions like Hinduism, Christianity, and Islam.In the course of time, they face marginalization in different forms, particularly becoming victims of land displacement, which hindered their livelihood.This article reviews various texts that trace this process of assimilation, integration, and consequent marginalization of the Agri and Koli communities.From tracing their origins in Mumbai to examining their current status in various literary texts, the article offers a brief primary-data review of both communities.
