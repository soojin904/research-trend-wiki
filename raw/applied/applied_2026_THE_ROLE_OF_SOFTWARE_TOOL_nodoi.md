## 초록

Teaching English using software tools fosters the development of students' professional communicative competence and expands opportunities to cultivate intellectual competencies—specifically research skills, independence, creative thinking, cognitive engagement, critical reasoning, and the ability to draft and review essays. Most importantly, this approach helps transform learners' attitudes toward the subject, facilitates the overcoming of language barriers among future educators, and supports the enhancement of communicative competence and effective interaction within the educational environment.
