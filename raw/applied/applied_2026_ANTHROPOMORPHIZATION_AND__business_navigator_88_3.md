# ANTHROPOMORPHIZATION AND DIALOGICITY IN UKRAINIAN BRANDS’ COMMUNICATION ON THREADS: A DUAL-INDEX ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGY

**저자**: Evgenia Konak
**출처**: Business Navigator No.5(88)
**발행일**: 2026-10-08
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.32782/business-navigator.88-3
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

The article examines the theoretical and applied aspects of anthropomorphization and dialogicity in the communication of Ukrainian brands on Threads. The study aims to distinguish between these interrelated but non-identical characteristics and to develop a methodological approach to their separate assessment. Anthropomorphization is interpreted as the use of verbal, emotional, and behavioral features through which a brand is perceived as a personified social actor, whereas dialogicity reflects the brand’s actual reciprocity in interaction with its audience. The distinction between anthropomorphization and brand voice is clarified: a recognizable brand voice may remain formal, neutral, or expert and does not necessarily contain human-like characteristics. The study proposes two complementary measures: the Brand Communication Anthropomorphization Index (BCAI) and the Brand Communication Dialogicity Index (BCDI). The BCAI comprises nine binary indicators grouped into verbal, emotional, and behavioral dimensions. The BCDI includes a dialogic stimulus, response coverage, contextual relevance of responses, and continuation of exchange. Separate calculation procedures are proposed for brand-initiated threads and externally initiated communication branches. A conceptual matrix combining the two indices is developed to identify four basic brand communication profiles. The application of the methodology is illustrated using publicly available Threads posts by Ukrainian brands. The examples demonstrate that a message with relatively few anthropomorphic features may initiate and sustain meaningful interaction, while a personified brand voice does not automatically indicate actual reciprocity. The findings confirm the analytical value of assessing brand voice and communication behavior separately. The proposed indices are aggregate measures; therefore, their interpretation should be supplemented by an analysis of individual indicators and components. The methodological approach can be used by digital marketing researchers, brand managers, social media specialists, and community management professionals to identify which aspect of communication requires improvement. The study has an illustrative rather than a large-scale empirical character. Further research should test intercoder reliability, establish empirically grounded thresholds, examine the appropriateness of different component weights, and assess the separate and combined associations of the two indices with audience engagement. It is also advisable to determine which anthropomorphic indicators and dialogicity components are most strongly associated with likes, meaningful user replies, reposts, quotes, and the continuation of communication threads.
