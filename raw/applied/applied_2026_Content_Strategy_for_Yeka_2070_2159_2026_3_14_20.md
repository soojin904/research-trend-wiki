# Content Strategy for Yekaterinburg Cultural Centers on the VKontakte Social Network: Evaluating Effectiveness through the Prism of User Engagement

**저자**: Nikolay V. OLUKHOV
**출처**: Culture management economics and law, pp.14-20
**발행일**: 2026-07-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.18572/2070-2159-2026-3-14-20
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of twelve Yekaterinburg cultural centers' content strategies on the «VKontakte» social network for the period 2024 - 2025. Engagement Rate (ER) and View Rate (VR) were used to analyze user activity levels. A reverse analysis of news feeds revealed a certain imbalance: high content visibility with low audience reactivity. Key drivers of success include: personalized birthday greetings that stimulate empathic responses through social identification mechanisms; biographical profiles of specialists that enhance trust through individuation; and contests and giveaways that activate gamification with a FOMO effect. In contrast, an analysis of «antitop » publications (political announcements, duplicate content, and a lack of endogenous multimedia) revealed factors that reduce engagement: cognitive dissonance, content fatigue, and algorithmic demography
