## 초록

Introduction: Domestic violence and violence against women represent serious human rights violations and constitute one of the major social challenges currently facing Brazil. High rates recorded nationwide are also reflected in the municipality where the Federal Institute of Sergipe (IFS), Socorro Campus, is located; this reality demands concrete actions to promote awareness, prevention, and efforts to combat this issue. Developing projects aimed at preventing domestic violence and violence against women is justified by the need to raise awareness among students, staff, and the entire school community regarding the gravity of the subject, as well as by the opportunities for social transformation that can arise from an education committed to human rights. Objectives: To contribute to knowledge regarding the Maria da Penha Law; to foster critical reflection among students, education professionals, and the school community; and to train educators while raising community awareness about violence in intimate relationships. Method: Interactive lectures on the Maria da Penha Law, statistical data on violence against women, and support mechanisms for women in situations of domestic and family violence. Conclusion: This topic is relevant to all audiences, ages, beliefs, and genders and must be addressed systematically to reduce interpersonal violence in Brazil; education is a key instrument for changing this scenario.
