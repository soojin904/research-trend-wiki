# Digital approaches to peripheral literary exchange

## 초록

Abstract This study examines the translation and circulation of Portuguese literature in China from 1942 to 2022, employing digital humanities methodologies including Social Network Analysis and Geographic Information Systems. Through analysis of the Catálogo de Autores Portugueses Publicados na China , this work identifies three major transformations: from state-controlled to market-driven publishing, from centralized to decentralized networks, and from collective to individual translation initiatives. The integration of computational methods reveals previously unrecognized patterns in peripheral-to-peripheral literary exchange (i.e., circulation between culturally and politically non-dominant regions), drawing on world-system theories of translation ( Heilbron 1999 ).These findings challenge traditional center-periphery models by showing that sustainable literary exchange between peripheral systems depends not solely on institutional support but on the alignment of market mechanisms, educational infrastructure, and individual initiative.
