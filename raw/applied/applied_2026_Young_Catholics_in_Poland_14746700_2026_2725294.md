## 초록

This article explores how bioethical liberalization among Polish adolescents relates to religious socialization and perceptions of religion’s normative role. Using Richard Osmer’s fourfold model of practical theology, we conducted a quantitative analysis on a representative sample of 1,029 secondary school students. Findings show selective acceptance of liberal bioethical views and a shift from religion as a normative authority toward a more relational-existential role. The proposed normativity index (NI) offers insight into these transformations. Osmer’s model enables theological reflection that is empirically grounded, dialogical, and attentive to value shifts among youth in a pluralistic society.
