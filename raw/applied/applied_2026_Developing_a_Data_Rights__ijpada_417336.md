# Developing a Data Rights Confirmation and Registration Model for Efficient Data Element Circulation

## 초록

Following the Chinese government's introduction of the “three rights separation of data” property rights scheme, data rights confirmation and registration (DRCR) has become crucial for addressing ownership issues and unlocking data value. This study systematically examines DRCR systems across 31 provincial regions, analyzing achievements, challenges, and regional differences. It introduces an innovative model for DRCR, categorizing and confirming public, enterprise, and personal data, while emphasizing public data's characteristics and application value. The model comprises a basic framework, data rights bundles, and core elements. Additionally, it incorporates standardization of DRCR and governance of ethical norms. From a holistic governance perspective, this study highlights the necessity and methods for implementing DRCR at whole data life cycle stages so that the previously fragmented stages are seamlessly connected and coordinated. This model's proposal and application will lay a crucial foundation for standardizing and maximizing the value of data element circulation.
