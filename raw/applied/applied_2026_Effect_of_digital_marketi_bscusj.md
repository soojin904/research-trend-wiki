## 초록

Digital marketing has become central to the way pay television providers reach and retain subscribers, yet many firms find it difficult to convert online engagement into genuine customer satisfaction. This study examined the influence of two digital marketing practices, social media marketing and content marketing, on customer satisfaction among Digital Satellite Television subscribers in Nigeria. A cross sectional survey design was adopted. The population comprised 2,345 active subscribers
