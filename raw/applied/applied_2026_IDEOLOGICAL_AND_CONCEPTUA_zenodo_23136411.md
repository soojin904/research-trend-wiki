## 초록

This article examines the ideological foundations of protecting young people’s minds from information threats in the context of globalization. It analyzes the main forms of contemporary information threats, including disinformation, manipulative content, radical ideas, cyberbullying, and other forms of destructive information influence. Particular attention is paid to the impact of digital technologies, social networks, and the global information environment on the formation of young people’s worldview and value orientations. The article substantiates the importance of developing critical thinking, media literacy, information culture, and ideological resilience among young people. It emphasizes the role of cooperation among families, educational institutions, mass media, civil society organizations, and the state in ensuring the information security of the younger generation. The article also proposes practical approaches aimed at strengthening young people’s resilience to manipulative and destructive information influence.
