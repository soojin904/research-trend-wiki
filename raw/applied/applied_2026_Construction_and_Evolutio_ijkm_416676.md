## 초록

The core texts of American Transcendentalism, especially the representative works of Emerson and Thoreau, remain central to intellectual history and literary studies, yet their conceptual architecture requires further systematic exploration. Using text mining methods—including keyword identification, co-word analysis, and topic clustering—this study constructs an American Transcendentalism Knowledge Graph (KG) to uncover conceptual structures and relational patterns across texts. The findings reveal distinctive thematic clusters and stage-oriented shifts in ideological evolution. While digital humanities tools offer new analytical perspectives, they must be grounded in original textual contexts. This research provides structural empirical evidence for Transcendentalist studies and demonstrates a replicable pathway for integrating text mining into literary and intellectual history.
