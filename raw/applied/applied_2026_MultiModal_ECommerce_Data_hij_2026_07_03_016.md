# Multi-Modal E-Commerce Data Analysis Integrating Visual Features and Text Comments

**저자**: Jing Tong; Wei Liu
**출처**: HighTech and Innovation Journal, Vol.7 No.3, pp.1077-1093
**발행일**: 2026-09-01
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.28991/hij-2026-07-03-016
**수집 키워드**: text analysis

## 초록

With the booming development of e-commerce, existing research analyzes visual or textual information in isolation from massive multi-modal data, ignoring the deep connections between modalities. This study builds a multi-modal e-commerce data analysis framework that integrates product visual features and user text comments. First, this study proposes a cascaded partial decoder network for product image saliency detection and builds a product saliency evaluation model that integrates aesthetic information, cascaded partial decoder-Res2Ne-saliency-augmented multi-pattern pooling (CPD-Res2Net-SMAP) to quantify visual appeal. Second, this study uses a large-scale language model to conduct high-precision sentiment analysis of e-commerce reviews and designs a multi-modal e-commerce data analysis system combined with multi-modal fusion strategies. Experimental results showed that the proposed cascaded partial decoder had superior performance on the saliency detection task, and the mean absolute error was as low as 0.042 on the self-built dataset. The CPD-Res2Net-SMAP model could more accurately evaluate the aesthetic quality of product images. The Spearman's rank correlation coefficient between its predicted score and human subjective score reached 0.768. The large model sentiment analysis method performed excellently when dealing with complex and ambiguous comments, with an accuracy rate as high as 94.8%. The integrated product information quality score had a strong positive correlation with core business indicators such as click-through rate and conversion rate (correlation coefficient was 0.78). This research provides new technical paths and theoretical support for e-commerce platforms to achieve refined operations, optimize user experience, and enhance commercial value.
