# Meta-Mathematics in The Holy Quran: Open Problems and Prospects

## 초록

The intersection of formal mathematical logic, computational linguistics, and sacred text analysis represents a rapidly evolving frontier in interdisciplinary research. This paper explores the application of meta-mathematical frameworks to the structural, phonetic, and semantic layers of the Holy Quran. By leveraging advanced concepts from algorithmic information theory, such as Kolmogorov complexity, and mapping rigorous phonetic recitation rules to formal axiomatic systems, we propose a novel methodology for quantifying the text's internal consistency and informational density. Furthermore, we draw metaphorical and structural parallels from complex physical and mathematical systems, including boundary conformal field theory, nuclear chirality, and polynomial automorphisms, to conceptualize the multi-layered symmetries inherent in the Quranic text. Through a hypothetical evaluation plan utilizing recent advancements in deep learning and phonetic scripting, this study establishes a theoretical foundation for future computational theology. The proposed framework highlights significant open problems in formalizing natural language phenomena into effectively inseparable logical systems.
