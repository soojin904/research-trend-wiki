# Economic and Cultural Impacts of Geographical Indications on Local Communities in Jammu and Kashmir: Evidence from a Mixed Methods Study

**발행일**: 2026-09-26

## 초록

This study examines the economic and cultural impacts of geographical indications (GI) on local communities in Jammu and Kashmir using a mixed-methods research design. GIs are intellectual property instruments that aim to protect the origin of products and promote regional development. The research design used is an exploratory mixed-methods design. The qualitative phase includes interviews and focus group discussions with producers, consumers, and stakeholders to examine the economic and cultural implications of GI certification. The quantitative phase uses survey data from 682 consumers, along with structural equation modelling, to test the determinants of consumer purchase intention for GI products. The analysis of the qualitative data reveals four major themes. These include increased market access and demand, improvement in product quality and standards, strengthening of community identity, and reinforcement of social and cultural networks. The quantitative results showed that perceived quality, perceived uniqueness, cultural significance, and consumer awareness significantly affect the purchase intention of consumer of GI-certified products. The research adds to the geographical indication literature by combining a cultural heritage perspective with consumer perceptions in an emerging regional setting. The findings raise awareness of the importance of geographical indications as a means that combines both the roles of protecting regional economic development and traditional knowledge systems. The results also offer some policy implications for enhancing consumer awareness and market opportunities for GI-based products.
