# 4 A Moment that Connects: Ephemeral Hashtags as Tools of Affective Community in Poland, the USA, and Russia

**저자**: Aleksandra Klimkiewicz; Marcin Trendowicz
**출처**: Digilit, pp.77-94
**발행일**: 2026-09-28

## 초록

This paper presents a comparative analysis of the affective and discursive functions of hashtags used in Polish, American, and Russian digital communication spheres.The research material includes protest, equality, solidarity, and propaganda hashtags.The study aims to demonstrate how ephemeral linguistic units organize emotions, build communities, and shape political and social narratives across three distinct infospheres.Qualitative discourse analysis was applied, supported by elements of cognitive linguistics and cultural semiotics.This methodological framework enabled the identification of three main functional categories of hashtags and the determination of mechanisms for the affective structuring of digital reality, such as the condensation of emotions, the compression of complex narrative content, the creation of micro-ideological structures, and the initiation of interpretive communities.The analysis shows that, although hashtags play different roles in each examined context, they simultaneously constitute a universal tool for participation in the affective public sphere.
