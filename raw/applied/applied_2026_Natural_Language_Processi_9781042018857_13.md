## 초록

This chapter examines the growing role of natural language processing (NLP) and sentiment analysis in economics and finance within increasingly digital and data-intensive environments. The chapter discusses how unstructured textual data from financial news, social media platforms, corporate disclosures, central bank communication, and policy documents can be transformed into quantitative insights using artificial intelligence and machine learning (ML) techniques. It explores the foundational concepts of NLP, including text preprocessing, tokenisation, ML, and deep learning, before examining sentiment analysis methods for measuring investor confidence, market sentiment, and economic uncertainty. The chapter further analyses practical applications of NLP in financial markets, macroeconomic forecasting, labour market analysis, and social media analytics. In addition, the chapter evaluates methodological, ethical, and computational challenges associated with NLP systems, including bias, interpretability, misinformation, and privacy concerns. The discussion highlights how NLP is reshaping economic forecasting, financial decision-making, and policy analysis in modern economies.
