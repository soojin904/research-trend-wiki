# Factors Influencing Grit among College Students: Focusing on SNS Addiction Tendency, Self-Control, and Impulsiveness

**저자**: Mi Kyoung Kang; In Kyoung Kim
**출처**: Asia-pacific Journal of Convergent Research Interchange, Vol.12 No.9, pp.909-921
**발행일**: 2026-09-28
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.47116/apjcri.2026.09.59
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study aimed to identify the effects of SNS addiction tendency, self-control, and impulsiveness on grit among college students and to provide basic data for developing educational and counseling interventions to enhance grit.A descriptive cross-sectional design was employed for this study.Participants were 218 college students recruited from three universities in Korea through convenience sampling.Data were collected using self-administered online questionnaires and analyzed using descriptive statistics, independent t-test, one-way ANOVA, Pearson's correlation coefficients, and hierarchical multiple regression analysis with SPSS Statistics 26.0.The results showed that SNS addiction tendency had significant negative correlations with self-control (r=-.442,p<.001) and grit (r=-.413,p<.001), while it was positively correlated with impulsiveness (r=.300, p<.001).Self-control demonstrated a significant positive correlation with grit (r=.702, p<.001) and a significant negative correlation with impulsiveness (r=-.566,p<.001), and impulsiveness was significantly and negatively correlated with grit (r=-.512,p<.001).Hierarchical multiple regression analysis revealed that selfcontrol (β=.499, p<.001), impulsiveness (β=-.218,p<.001), SNS addiction tendency (β=-.113,p=.025), sleep duration (β=.144, p=.004), academic major (β=.129, p=.013), and major satisfaction (β=.100, p=.033) were significant predictors of grit, and these variables explained 57.1% of the variance in grit.These findings suggest that strengthening self-control while reducing impulsiveness and SNS addiction tendency may substantially contribute to enhancing grit among college students.These findings suggest that enhancing self-control and reducing impulsiveness and SNS addiction may improve grit among college students.Universities should therefore implement evidence-based educational and counseling programs that promote self-regulation and healthy SNS use.Such programs may help students sustain long-term goals and adapt successfully to university life.
