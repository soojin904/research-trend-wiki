# INTEGRATION OF SOCIAL NETWORKS AND VR TECHNOLOGIES INTO THE EDUCATIONAL PROCESS AS A FACTOR IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF COMMUNICATIVE AND DIGITAL COMPETENCES

## 초록

This article analyses the pedagogical potential of social networks and VR technologies as tools for developing students' communicative and digital competences in the context of digitalisation and transformation of language teaching approaches.The relevance of the study stems from the fact that the modern educational process goes beyond the traditional classroom settings and acquires the characteristics of multimodal, interactive and socially oriented interaction, within which the English language functions not only as an educational subject but also as a tool for participation in digital practices.Social networks and VR environments create new conditions for the development of communicative competence.They recreate authentic contexts, provide real-time interaction, create unpredictable situations, and integrate linguistic, social, cognitive and technological aspects of activity.Social networks are viewed as multifunctional platforms for multimodal communication, within which students can create English-language content, participate in discussions, analyse information flows, identify manipulation strategies and develop skills in responsible digital behaviour.Particular attention is paid to the development of critical thinking and media literacy which ensure the ability to interpret, evaluate and produce complex English-language media texts.VR technologies, in turn, enable the simulation of foreign-language environments in which communication acquires a spatial and activity-based dimension.They allow you to recreate professional, everyday and intercultural situations with a high degree of realism, contributing to the development of strategic, pragmatic and non-verbal components of communicative competence.The interactive and adaptive nature of VR makes it possible to individualise the educational process and shape personalised trajectories for the development of communication skills, while creating conditions for mastering team interaction skills, finding joint solutions and responding appropriately to cultural markers as important signs of established intercultural communication.
