# Self-Esteem and Social Networking Addiction among College Students

**저자**: Annamma Tu
**출처**: International Journal of Science and Research (IJSR), pp.592-595
**발행일**: 2026-10-12
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.21275/mr261009053417
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

The present study examined the relationship between self-esteem and social networking addiction among college students and investigated differences across gender and educational level. A descriptive research design was used with 210 participants from Kerala selected through an unrestricted self-selected survey method. The sample included 69 males and 141 females; 149 were undergraduate students, 27 were postgraduate students, and 34 were professional degree students. Data were collected using the Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, the Social Networking Addiction Scale, and a socio-demographic data sheet. Pearson product-moment correlation, independent-samples t-test, and one-way ANOVA were used for statistical analysis. The dissertation reports a significant positive correlation between self-esteem and social networking addiction (r = .44, p < .01). Significant gender differences were reported for self-esteem and social networking addiction. No significant differences were reported across undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional degree groups for either variable. The findings highlight the relevance of understanding social networking behaviour and self-evaluative processes among college students. The study also identifies the need for broader samples and more controlled data-collection procedures in future research.
