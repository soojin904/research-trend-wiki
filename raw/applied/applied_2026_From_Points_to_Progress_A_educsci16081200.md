# From Points to Progress: A Systematic Literature Review on How Gamification Transforms Engagement, Motivation, and Learning Outcomes in Distance Education

## 초록

This literature review examines how gamification influences engagement, motivation, and learning outcomes in distance education. As online learning environments often face challenges related to student isolation and diminished motivation, gamification—the integration of game-design elements into non-game contexts—emerges as a strategic pedagogical tool. Following PRISMA guidelines, this study synthesizes 70 peer-reviewed articles, analyzing key mechanics such as points, leaderboards, and badges, alongside psychological and cognitive frameworks including Self-Determination Theory, Flow Theory, and Cognitive Load Theory. The findings indicate that while gamification positively influences student participation and emotional investment, its effectiveness is highly context-dependent, requiring strict pedagogical alignment and the avoidance of an over-reliance on extrinsic rewards (“pointsification”). Furthermore, the review highlights the role of personalized feedback, cognitive scaffolding, and social interaction in sustaining progress. The results suggest that when integrated thoughtfully, gamification can mitigate online attrition and foster a more resilient, interactive distance learning ecosystem. This work concludes by identifying gaps in current research, particularly regarding methodologically limited long-term cognitive outcomes, the novelty effect, and the necessity for instructor training in gamified methodologies.
