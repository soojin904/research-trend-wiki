# Grounding vs. Prompt Engineering for Compute-Constrained Vision-Language Models in AI Product Demos

**저자**: N. K. Jijith; Loveleen Gaur; R. Meenakshi; Varsha Arya; Ramakrishnan Raman
**출처**: International Journal of Software Science and Computational Intelligence, Vol.18 No.1, pp.1-28
**발행일**: 2026-09-30
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4018/ijssci.423390
**수집 키워드**: text analysis

## 초록

Small vision-language models can generate product demonstration scripts but may assert attributes unsupported by product records. This study compares metadata grounding and prompt engineering across 900 generations from two open-weight models (500 million and two billion parameters), using 150 Amazon Berkeley Objects products in a paired within-product design. Supplying verified catalog attributes more than doubled coverage—from 0.300 to 0.641 for the larger model and from 0.239 to 0.613 for the smaller—with paired effect sizes of 1.19 and 1.14. An explicit output scaffold produced much smaller effects of 0.21 and 0.18. Conditionally on usable output, the smaller model matched the larger on coverage. Because coverage uses normalized string matching and unsupported assertions are detected with a closed lexicon, both measures are approximations. The results show that catalog integration, rather than prompt refinement, is the determinant of factual accuracy in this setting.
