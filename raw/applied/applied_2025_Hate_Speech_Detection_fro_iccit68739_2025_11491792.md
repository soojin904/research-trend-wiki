## 초록

People spend a large number time on social media or in the virtual world. As a result of these traditional advances in technology, people's daily life has become more dynamic and the world is now at their fingertips for people's long-distance communication. Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram are the most used social media to keep in touch with relatives and friends far and wide. Some unwanted incidents happen all the time through this online communication. Many are victims of cyberbullying in cyberspace and organized cybercrime. Adopting modern technological support to create secure cyberspace can be a groundbreaking step. Deep learning and machine learning now working on building prediction, detection, and recognition model. Significant work on machine learning and planning for text analysis and recognition has been completed and is being elaborated. This study has published a proposal to create a model using deep learning for hate speech detection from text. Text data is processed in two different ways and deep learning algorithms are used separately for each method. Among the algorithms used in both methods, RNN provided relatively good accuracy and its MSE was lower than the others.
