# Succeeding at all Cost: Social Perceptions, Family Pressure, and Baccalaureate Cheating Practices in Duékoué

## 초록

Exam cheating at the baccalaureate level undermines the quality of Ivorian education and affects social cohesion. Despite measures implemented by the State, cases persist every year, particularly in Duékoué, where multiple actors are involved. This study seeks to understand how social perceptions and family pressures influence baccalaureate cheating practices in the commune of Duékoué. Adopting a qualitative interpretive approach, the study was conducted through interviews with fifty-six (56) respondents selected according to the principle of saturation. The findings show that social realities, representations of the diploma, and family pressures largely account for the prevalence of cheating and constrain the effectiveness of ministerial strategies. It is therefore necessary to integrate these social dimensions into anti-cheating policies in order to enhance their relevance and operational effectiveness
