# Augmented reality as a digital scaffold for slow travel: The roles of cultural competence and tourist engagement at a heritage site

**저자**: Jiayu Xie; Xi Li; Xijia Huang; Ling Zhang; Lulu Cheng; Zixin Feng
**출처**: Journal of Destination Marketing & Management, Vol.43, pp.101166-101166
**발행일**: 2026-09-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdmm.2026.101166
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Heritage sites are designed to foster in-depth cultural experiences for tourists at destinations and are well suited for promoting slow travel. However, cultural differences and tourist’ limited awareness often hinder its practice. While augmented reality (AR) is typically used to provide immediate destination information, its potential to build cultural competence and influence slow travel intentions has not been examined. Grounded in zone of proximal development theory, this study reveals that the perceived enjoyment of AR is positively associated with cultural competence, but only for highly engaged users. For less engaged users, personal innovativeness moderates the relationship between perceived advantage and cultural competence. Across both groups, cultural competence strengthens both empathy and slow travel intentions. These findings offer guidance for implementing slow travel initiatives at cultural heritage sites.
