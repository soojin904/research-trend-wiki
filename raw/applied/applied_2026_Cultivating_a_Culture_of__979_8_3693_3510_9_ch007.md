## 초록

This chapter, cultivating a culture of innovation with AI, explores how entrepreneurial leaders can leverage Artificial Intelligence to become more innovative amid ethical issues. It confirms three overarching research objectives: to illustrate how leaders can use AI to create innovation-enabling cultures that solve ethical quandaries; to define AI applications and solutions with proven effectiveness in entrepreneurial scenarios and how they can be extended; and to chart the future trends that define the role of AI in entrepreneurship and the strategic-cultural change that leaders should undergo. The study uses a descriptive and exploratory computational text-analysis design by assembling a 2014-2024 corpus using Scopus, Web of Science and ACM and using bibliometric mapping and topic modelling and validating the relevance of themes using semantic coherence and expert interpretation.
