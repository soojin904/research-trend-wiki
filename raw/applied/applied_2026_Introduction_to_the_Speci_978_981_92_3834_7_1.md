## 초록

This chapter introduces the species of cynomolgus monkey ( Macaca fascicularis ) in the context of nonhuman primates and the genus Macaca . It first outlines the general features, distribution, and classification of nonhuman primates and Old World monkeys, followed by an overview of macaques, including their phylogeny, evolutionary history, and species groups. The main focus is on cynomolgus monkeys, covering subspecies classification, morphological and anatomical characteristics, sensory perception, communication, social behavior, geographical and ecological distribution, habitat, feeding habits, reproduction, population status, conservation level, and relevant regulations in China. The chapter highlights the close biological similarity of cynomolgus monkeys to humans and their value as a nonhuman primate model in biomedical research and drug safety evaluation. It also emphasizes the high genetic diversity of the species, the importance of geographical origin, and the need for scientific breeding, population monitoring, habitat protection, and sustainable utilization. This chapter provides a useful reference for researchers, breeders, laboratory animal staff, and regulatory personnel.
