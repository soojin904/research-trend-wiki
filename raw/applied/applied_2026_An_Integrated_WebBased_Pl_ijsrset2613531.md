# An Integrated Web-Based Platform for Student Engagement, Campus Communities and Alumni Interaction

## 초록

Digital platforms nowadays have an important role to play in the way that students communicate and interact in higher education, but student interaction, campus communities, and alumni networking are generally carried out on separate platforms. In order to tackle this issue, the paper introduces Hive, a web-based platform that combines all these activities into a single environment focused on the institution. Hive provides features such as user authentication, role-based access control, social posts, comments, likes, bookmarks, campus communities, alumni profiles, professional opportunities, mentorship interactions, direct messaging, notifications, and search. The frontend is built using React and Vite, and the backend makes use of Node.js and Express.js together with MongoDB and Mongoose for data management. The platform also has JWT-based authentication, password hashing, and server-side authorization to safeguard user accounts and protected functions. This study is concerned with the design and implementation of Hive and demonstrates how student interaction, campus communities, alumni networking, and direct communication can be incorporated into one system. Since the current work does not include user-based evaluation, it does not claim improvements in student engagement or satisfaction. Future work will focus on evaluating usability, adoption, engagement, and interaction through user feedback and system usage data.
