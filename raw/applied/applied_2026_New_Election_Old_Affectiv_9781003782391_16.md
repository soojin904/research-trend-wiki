## 초록

This research examines how cyber troop operations in the 2024 Presidential Election played a role in reinforcing, changing, and intensifying the affective polarisation that began to take shape in the 2019 Election. It also examines how cyber troop strategies and tactics in 2024 shaped and were influenced by the existing affective polarisation, as well as the factors that influenced continuity or changes in cyber troop strategies between the 2019 and 2024 elections. Affective polarisation – a condition in which political supporters not only differ in their views, but also display extreme emotional attitudes towards opposing groups – is the main focus of this study. The method in this study uses social network analysis (SNA) on social media Twitter (X) during the 2019 and 2024 elections and in-depth interviews with 46 members of the cyber army. The results indicate that affective polarisation is not only maintained but also actively produced by the cyber army through campaigns geared towards creating a sense of emotional attachment towards candidates as well as hatred towards political opponents. In this case, social media algorithms play a crucial role in amplifying emotional and provocative content, creating a digital echo chamber that further deepens fragmentation. Meanwhile, the state indirectly benefits politically from this situation, as it mobilises a support base through emotional loyalty, and marginalises moderate or critical voices that do not fit the dominant narrative. Affective polarisation – in this framework – becomes a strategic tool that is continuously reproduced to serve the interests of power in a hyperactive and fragmented digital landscape.
