# Factors Affecting Learning Outcomes of Heritage-Based Red Study Tours in Blended Second-Class Big Data Education

**저자**: Yanna Huang; Luxi Chen
**출처**: International Journal of Mobile and Blended Learning, Vol.18 No.1, pp.1-17
**발행일**: 2026-10-12
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.4018/ijmbl.424463
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study addresses the limitations of conventional red study tour evaluations, which overly rely on questionnaires and qualitative observation and lack whole-process behavioral analysis and causal evidence. Based on multi-source heterogeneous data including campus card records, LMS logs, on-site GPS trajectories at red heritage sites, and anonymous surveys, a four-dimensional evaluation system covering academic, behavioral, affective, and practical learning outcomes is constructed. The Delphi method is used to determine indicator weights, and propensity score matching with Bootstrap confidence intervals is employed to eliminate selection bias and validate blended intervention effects. An interpretable XGBoost-LSTM model with SHAP analysis is applied to identify key influencing factors and underlying mechanisms. The results indicate that the integration of online learning, on-site heritage investigation, and red story creation significantly improves students' comprehensive learning outcomes, with social network centrality playing a vital mediating role.
