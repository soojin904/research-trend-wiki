# Social Networks and Unequal Income Returns to Education: Gender Differences and Cross-National Context

**저자**: Emmi Mäkeläinen; Aki Koivula
**출처**: 
**발행일**: 2026-09-29

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study examines how social networks are associated with unequal income returns to education and whether these associations vary by gender and across national contexts. Using data from the International Social Survey Programme (ISSP) 2017 from representative samples in 15 countries, the study analyses the interrelations between educational attainment, social ties, and personal income. The analyses distinguish both the status of social contacts and the type of ties to the upper middle class, while also examining variation by gender and country-level income inequality. The results show that access to upper-middle-class contacts is associated with wider educational differences in income, and that close ties, especially family members, relatives, and close friends, are more strongly associated with income than weaker ties. These patterns also vary by gender, with educated men’s income more strongly associated with upper-middle-class ties than educated women’s. In addition, educational differences in the association between social networks and income are more pronounced in countries with higher levels of income inequality. The study contributes to research on social stratification by showing that income returns to education are socially embedded and also vary by gender and national context.
