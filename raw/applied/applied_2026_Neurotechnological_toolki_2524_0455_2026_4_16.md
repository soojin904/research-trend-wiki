# Neurotechnological toolkit for sales management of intermediary commercial structures in social media

**저자**: Olha Ye. Haponenko; Tetiana M. Kutsenko; Yuliia Tataryntseva
**출처**: Actual problems of innovative economy and law, Vol.2026 No.4, pp.95-99

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This article develops an integrative conceptual toolkit for sales management by intermediary commercial structures operating in social media, combining the classical sales process framework with neurotechnological methods of consumer behavior diagnostics. The study employs systematic analysis of prior empirical research, comparative analysis, and conceptual modeling. Intermediary commercial structures increasingly rely on social media not only for product promotion but for the full sales cycle, from prospecting to post-purchase engagement. However, conventional behavioral metrics such as clicks, impressions, and conversion rates fail to capture the underlying cognitive and affective mechanisms driving purchase decisions at each stage of the sales process. At the same time, academic coverage of neuroscience applications in sales management remains substantially narrower than in consumer marketing, and no integrative framework currently links established sales-process stage models with neurotechnological diagnostic methods for the specific context of intermediary commercial structures acting through social media channels. The article proposes an integrative matrix that maps the seven stages of the classical sales process onto the three core social selling activities and further onto the appropriate neurotechnological instruments (electroencephalography, functional magnetic resonance imaging, galvanic skin response, eye-tracking and brain-computer interfaces), grounded in real predictive-validity and adoption-barrier evidence from prior empirical studies. Practical selection criteria are formulated: instrument choice should depend on the specific sales stage and diagnostic task rather than on a generic preference for neurotechnology, and instrument adoption should be evaluated jointly through reliability and predictive validity rather than reliability alone. The scientific novelty lies in the first systematic linkage of the sales-process stage model, operationalized social selling activities, and neurotechnological diagnostic methods for intermediary commercial structures. Sales managers can use the results to select diagnostic tools by sales stage. Further research should empirically test the matrix in campaigns of Ukrainian intermediary commercial structures. Keywords: sales management, social media, neuromarketing, intermediary commercial structures, social selling, sales process, neurotechnology.
