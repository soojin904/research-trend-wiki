# A study on the activation of agency and niche construction among Generation Z in the dissemination of central plains culture

**저자**: Yu Liu
**출처**: Advances in Social Behavior Research, Vol.17 No.10, pp.142-152
**발행일**: 2026-09-28

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Generation Z is emerging as an active agent in the field of cultural communication as digital natives. However, systematic empirical research remains lacking on how their agency is activated and how they construct distinctive niches in the dissemination of Central Plains culture. This study adopts a combination of case study and content analysis, taking local communication practices in Henan as the core objects of analysis and constructing an interconnected analytical framework of "agency activation–niche construction." The analysis shows that the activation of Generation Z's agency is not a linear progression, but rather results from the intertwined and mutually constitutive effects of four driving forces: "emotional triggering–value affirmation–community connection–technological empowerment." On this basis, Generation Z establishes a distinctive "mediating" niche that differs from those of official institutions and traditional actors through three pathways: the content niche, relational niche, and technological niche. In response to tensions including insufficient sustainability, the Matthew effect of algorithms, and the erosion of cultural substance through excessive entertainment, this study proposes a collaborative ecological construction model centered on "platforms–government–universities" and supplemented by improvements in young people's media literacy. This model provides a theoretical reference for the "dual-track" approach to regional cultural communication.
