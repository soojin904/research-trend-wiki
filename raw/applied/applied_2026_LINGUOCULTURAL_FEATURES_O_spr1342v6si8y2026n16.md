## 초록

This article presents a comparative study of the linguocultural features of personal identity construction in English and Uzbek Internet discourse. The empirical data comprise social media profiles, user biographies, Telegram and Instagram posts, and forms of self-presentation in the YouTube environment. Through qualitative and comparative analysis, the study examines how personal identity is constructed through family and kinship relations, social roles, personal values, emotional states, and symbolic and figurative representations. The findings demonstrate both common patterns and distinctive linguocultural tendencies associated with cultural values and social relationships in the two discourse contexts.
