# Intergenerational Educational Mobility and Self-Rated Health Among Chinese Older Adults: The Mediating Role of Children’s Financial Support and Social Networks

**저자**: Wang Ping; Yimeng Feng; Chen Wu

## 초록

BackgroundThe relationship between intergenerational educational mobility and older adults' health remains underexplored.ObjectiveTo investigate the link between intergenerational educational mobility and self-rated health among Chinese older adults, and to explore the mediation of children's financial support and social networks.MethodsWe analyzed four waves (2014-2020) of longitudinal data from the China Longitudinal Aging Social Survey (CLASS) (13,033 observations) using individual growth models and generalized structural equation modeling.ResultsCompared with the low educational stability group, both upward mobility and high educational stability were associated with better self-rated health among older adults. Children's financial support and older adults' social networks partially mediated these associations. Downward mobility was only indirectly associated with health through social networks.
