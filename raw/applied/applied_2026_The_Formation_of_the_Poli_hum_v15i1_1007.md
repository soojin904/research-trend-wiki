## 초록

The aim of the paper is to analyze NATO's policy and strategy from the founding of the alliance to the present day, with a focus onthe situation between the alliance's American and European partners during Donald Trump's second presidency.The topic discusses the main reasons for the growth of disagreements between the United States and several European states, whilealso emphasizing that NATO remains a strong alliance that continues to play an important role in today's world politics.
