## 초록

Abstract This document formally introduces the concept of the Neutral Structural Text Reflector (NSTR) — a system designed to represent the structural properties of textual data without semantic interpretation, inference, or evaluative processing. The purpose of this publication is to establish a formal, non-interpretative framework for structural text analysis and to publicly fix authorship and conceptual priority of the NSTR construct. The system described herein operates exclusively on structural features such as tokenization, grammatical relations, and formal patterns. It explicitly excludes semantic reasoning, meaning attribution, evaluation, or decision-making. This document serves as a conceptual and definitional reference. No implementation, derivative work, or applied usage is authorized without explicit permission from the author.
