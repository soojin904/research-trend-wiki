# AI Customer Service, Perceived Diagnosticity, and Brand Loyalty in Cosmetics Live-Streaming Commerce

## 초록

In China's cosmetics live-streaming commerce, AI customer service is evolving from a tool for automated replies into a digital service touchpoint that shapes product assessments, service evaluations, and brand relationships.Because cosmetics are experiential products that carry pronounced product-fit uncertainty, consumers attend not only to response speed but also to whether AI customer service helps them assess a product's fit for their skin type, functional needs, shade preference, and usage context.Drawing on the stimulusorganism-response (S-O-R) framework and uncertainty-reduction theory, this study develops a serial-mediation model linking AI customer service experience, perceived diagnosticity, trust, satisfaction, and brand loyalty, and tests it with partial least squares structural equation modelling (PLS-SEM) using survey data from 450 Chinese consumers of cosmetics livestreaming commerce.The results indicate that AI customer service experience is positively associated with perceived diagnosticity, which is in turn associated with greater trust in the brand and its service information.Trust is associated with brand loyalty both directly and indirectly through satisfaction, and the bias-corrected bootstrap estimates are consistent with sequential mediation through perceived diagnosticity, trust, and satisfaction.These findings suggest that, by supplying tailored decision support that reduces product-fit uncertainty, AI customer service can help convert brief service interactions into stronger brand preference.The study positions AI customer service as an integral element of the digital service systems that underpin live-commerce platforms and offers operational guidance for deploying it as a decision-support mechanism rather than a mere cost-saving channel.
