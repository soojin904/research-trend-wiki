# Toward sustainable student support in digital education: A phenomenological study of technology and AI-supported counselling practice

**저자**: Patricia Mawusi Amos
**출처**: Sustainable Education and Digital Transformation
**발행일**: 2026-09-27
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.33902/sedt.202645132
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

The study explored how student counsellors integrate digital technologies and Artificial Intelligence (AI) into their practicum experience. Using an exploratory phenomenological design, data were collected through semi-structured interviews with fifteen postgraduate counselling students at the University of Education, Winneba. The study examined the types of tools used, perceived benefits, challenges encountered and the implications for the counselling relationship. Findings indicate that student counsellors frequently rely on basic digital tools for communication, documentation and preparation while AI applications are used more selectively to support planning, idea generation and case conceptualization. Participants reported that these technologies improved efficiency, enhanced preparation, and increased confidence before sessions. However, several challenges emerged including limited infrastructure, unreliable internet access, lack of formal training and concerns about the cultural relevance of AI-generated content. Importantly, participants maintained clear boundaries by avoiding the use of AI during live counselling sessions, emphasizing the need to preserve empathy, trust and relational depth. The study highlights the growing role of technology in counsellor training while underscoring the importance of ethical awareness, contextual sensitivity and human centred practice. It recommends the integration of AI literacy, ethical guidelines and improved digital infrastructure into counsellor education to better prepare trainees for contemporary practice.
