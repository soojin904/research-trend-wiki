## 초록

Symmetry Lifting Framework The core tenet of the symmetry lifting framework is to map low-dimensional, low-symmetry physical systems to static reference spacetimes of high dimension and elevated symmetry via isometric embedding (or the more general categorical lifting). As a result, intricate many-body dynamics, nonequilibrium transport, and turbulence problems are recast as computationally tractable geometric problems in high-dimensional geometry—including geodesic lengths, minimal surface areas, max-flow/min-cut on graph networks, and shortest-path trees. This study systematically constructs a unified theoretical architecture spanning weak to strong symmetry breaking, continuous analytical to discrete data-driven methodologies, fundamental holography to engineering implementations, as well as fluid mechanics and weather forecasting. Part I: Analytical Geometric Framework for Weakly Symmetry-Broken Systems We rigorously prove that any two-dimensional real-analytic Riemannian manifold permits isometric embedding into four-dimensional (or higher) symmetric manifolds with cyclic coordinates, and establish a complete mathematical formalism for extending local embeddings to global ones via Cartan–Kähler theory, the Cauchy–Kowalevski theorem, and path lifting. Building upon this foundation, we derive 35 core analytical formulas governing canonical problems including AdS/CFT, dS/CFT, holographic superconductors, quantum chaos, and the black hole information paradox: the anisotropic primordial power spectrum, tensor-to-scalar ratio r=16\varepsilon, non-Gaussianity parameter f_{\text{NL}}=\frac{5}{12}(1-n_s), saturated Lyapunov exponent \Lambda_L=2\pi T_H, direction-dependent butterfly velocity, and the full analytical expression for the Page curve in dynamical black hole evaporation T_P\approx\frac{3\beta_0}{2\pi c}S_{\text{BH}}(0), alongside a universal electromagnetic response formula \sigma(\Omega)=\frac{iM_{\text{eff}}^2}{\Omega}. Here the effective mass squared M_{\text{eff}}^2=C_s\psi_h^2+C_c\phi_h^2+C_{\text{sc}}\psi_h^2\phi_h^2 uniformly characterizes s-wave, p-wave, d-wave, FFLO states, and competing phases of superconductivity and charge density waves. Part II: Categorical and Discrete Geometric Framework for Strongly Symmetry-Broken Systems For regimes of severe symmetry breaking—such as disorder, lattice defects, finite temperatures, and nonequilibrium driving—we combine moduli tensor classification with flat functors to lift boundary tensor networks into bulk weighted graphs in higher dimensions. Rigorous proofs yield the following key results: 1. The topological entanglement entropy \gamma=\ln\mathcal{D} equals the minimal cut of Kitaev–Preskill composite partitions on the bulk network; 2. Two-point correlation functions take the form \langle\mathcal{O}(P)\mathcal{O}(Q)\rangle=e^{-\Delta L_{\text{min}}(P,Q)}, governed by the shortest geodesic path length between operators, while multi-point correlators correspond to the total weight of minimal Steiner trees; 3. The R-matrix for anyon braiding statistics is derived from the disparity in shortest-path lengths before and after worldline exchange, and the S-matrix is fixed by minimal cuts on toroidal boundaries, complying with the Verlinde formula; 4. The finite-temperature partition function admits a geometric representation Z(\beta)=\sum_e e^{-w_\beta(e)} with edge weight w_\beta(e)=-\ln d_{\text{Ze}}+\beta\varepsilon_e; phase transitions coincide with divergent correlation lengths on the graph, corresponding to anomalous diffusion scaling d_{\text{min}}<1; 5. Steady-state nonequilibrium electric and thermal currents are equivalent to max-flow/min-cut problems on the graph, with stochastic noise quantified by ensemble averaging over random weight distributions. This fully discrete framework operates independent of the continuum limit, with all computations executable via classical graph algorithms: Dijkstra’s algorithm, max-flow/min-cut solvers, and Steiner tree construction. Part III: Holographic Geometric Framework for Continuous Fluid Mechanics and Meteorology Starting from the shallow-water equations and incompressible Navier–Stokes equations, we construct high-dimensional AdS bulk metrics. We demonstrate that the Kolmogorov turbulence energy spectrum scaling E(k)\sim k^{-5/3} arises from two-point correlators with scaling dimension \delta=1/3 in the bulk geometry. Using gradient expansion, we rigorously derive the full Navier–Stokes system (including nonlinear advective terms) and demonstrate its equivalence to the constraint equations of Einstein gravity near the bulk horizon, where every transport coefficient is uniquely fixed by bulk geometric quantities. We leverage ERA5 reanalysis meteorological datasets to build boundary information-distance graphs, which are lifted into bulk graph networks through spectral clustering and coarse-graining operations. Atmospheric statistical correlators, energy fluxes, extreme event occurrence probabilities, and water vapor transport channels are respectively mapped to shortest paths, minimal cuts, generalized Pareto distributions, and maximum flows within the bulk network. Exact analytical solutions are derived from bulk scalar field dynamics, encompassing Poiseuille flow, the Stokes problem, Hartmann flow, power-law non-Newtonian fluids, Mathieu-type magnetohydrodynamic flows in inhomogeneous magnetic fields, and multiple self-similar nonlinear magnetohydrodynamic solutions. A novel exact steady-state solution to the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations is presented: U(x,y)=-\frac{6\nu}{y^2},\quad v(x,y)=-\frac{6\nu}{y},\quad p(x,y)=-\frac{12\rho\nu^2}{y^2}\quad (y>0) along with its dual holographic counterpart. Part IV: Financial and Social Network Applications The symmetry lifting framework is extended to quantitative finance and social network analytics. Equity markets or social interaction networks are modeled as boundary graphs endowed with information distances defined by pairwise correlation or interaction strength. Spectral clustering and coarse-graining lift these boundary graphs into high-dimensional weighted bulk networks, where macroeconomic and sociological observables—systemic risk contagion, systemic importance metrics, and opinion polarization—are translated into shortest paths, minimal cuts, and maximum flows over the bulk graph. Geometric expressions are rigorously derived for financial correlation functions, risk entropy, liquidity propagation capacity, and opinion evolution dynamics. This framework furnishes geometric computational tools for systemic risk early warning, public sentiment monitoring, and targeted network intervention strategies. Unifying Significance This formalism unifies holographic duality, category theory, tensor networks, graph theory, and fluid dynamics to deliver a consistent geometric description of cross-scale physical phenomena ranging from quantum gravity to operational weather prediction. Every theoretical prediction is validated via numerical simulation or benchmarked against established experimental measurements, marking the comprehensive expansion of the symmetry lifting paradigm: spanning analytical treatments of weakly broken symmetry, discrete geometric methods for strong symmetry breaking, and translational applications from fundamental physics to engineering and Earth sciences.
