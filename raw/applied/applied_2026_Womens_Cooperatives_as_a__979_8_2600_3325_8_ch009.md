## 초록

Women's empowerment is a crucial element of economic and social development, contributing to gender equality and sustainable growth. This chapter examines the impact of women's cooperatives on economic and social empowerment, focusing on the case of the Burdur Ball Sewing Cooperative in Türkiye. Through qualitative research methods, including in-depth interviews with cooperative members, this study explores how cooperatives contribute to financial independence, expand social networks, and challenge traditional gender roles. The findings indicate that participation in women's cooperatives enhances economic self-sufficiency, strengthens social ties, and promotes gender equality. However, challenges such as financial constraints and social stereotypes persist, necessitating further policy support. The chapter concludes that women's cooperatives serve as an effective tool for social transformation and recommends expanding financial resources, educational programs, and cooperative networks to maximize their impact.
