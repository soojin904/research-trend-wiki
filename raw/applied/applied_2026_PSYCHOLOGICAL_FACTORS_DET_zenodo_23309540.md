# PSYCHOLOGICAL FACTORS DETERMINING THE STABILITY AND DISTRIBUTION OF STUDENTS' ATTENTION IN THE DIGITAL INFORMATION ENVIRONMENT

## 초록

This article theoretically analyzes the psychological factors that determine the stability and distribution of attention of students of higher educational institutions in the digital information environment. The widespread use of smartphones, computers, tablets, electronic learning platforms, social networks and messengers in the modern educational process significantly expands the opportunities for students to work with information The study identified the following key psychological factors that influence attention stability and distribution: learning motivation, self-regulation, volitional control, cognitive load, working memory capacity, emotional state, digital multitasking, digital distractions, time management, and digital hygiene. The article also developed practical recommendations for stabilizing students' attention and psychologically optimizing the digital information environment.
