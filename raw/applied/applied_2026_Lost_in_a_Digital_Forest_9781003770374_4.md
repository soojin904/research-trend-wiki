## 초록

This chapter assesses the special effects of technology on people with ADHD, and how these impact their couple relationships. The chapter utilises the expertise of the co-author Professor Yair Amichai-Hamburger to examine the features of the internet which make it particularly compelling to such people. These include the safety, anonymity, control, and fun that the internet environment provides. Moreover, the online design features, for example, rapid pacing, constant novelty, immediate rewards, notifications, and endless options, reinforce the ADHD tendency towards stimulation seeking. The chapter examines how FOMO leads to multitasking and examines whether people engaged in a high attention task can actually multitask and how their belief that they are doing so affects their intimate relationships. The chapter goes on to describe the psychological engineering of social networks as creators design sites to keep all users perpetually online, and how these tactics are particularly attuned to the ADHD brain wiring.
