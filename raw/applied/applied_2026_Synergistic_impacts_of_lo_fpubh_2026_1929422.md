# Synergistic impacts of low-carbon city pilot policy and information consumption pilot policy on carbon lock-in

**저자**: Yan Peng; Rongguang Zhang
**출처**: Frontiers in Public Health, Vol.14
**발행일**: 2026-10-09

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Introduction Carbon lock-in poses a significant barrier to the low-carbon transition, necessitating synergistic policy interventions. Methods Using a dual machine learning (DML) model, this study estimates carbon lock-in data for 262 Chinese cities and examines how the dual policy (the low-carbon city pilot and information consumption pilot policy) affects carbon lock-in. Results The dual policy significantly suppresses carbon lock-in, with a markedly stronger effect than implementing either policy in isolation. The analysis indicates that the dual policy works through industrial structure upgrading, government environmental support, climate technology innovation, and public climate concern. Additionally, the heterogeneity test reveals that the suppressive effect is more pronounced in non-resource-based cities, large cities, and those with high market vitality and strong financial regulation. Discussion Consequently, this study provides evidence for promoting the synergistic implementation of the low-carbon city pilot and the information consumption pilot policy, thereby breaking carbon lock-in.
