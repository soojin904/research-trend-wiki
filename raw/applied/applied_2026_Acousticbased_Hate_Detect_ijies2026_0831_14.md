# Acoustic-based Hate Detection in Dialectal Arabic Speech Using a Speech Lightweight Sequential Deep Learning Framework

**저자**: Noor Sahib Farhan; Matheel E. Abdulmunim; Hasanen S. Abdullah; Musheer Ahmad
**출처**: International journal of intelligent engineering and systems, Vol.19 No.8, pp.272-289
**발행일**: 2026-07-28
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.22266/ijies2026.0831.14
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Detecting hate speech in spoken language is not adequately researched relative to text-based methods, especially in low-resource and highly dialectal spoken-language settings, where the use of voice-based communication is common despite a shortage of speech datasets.This study proposes an acoustic-based system for multi hate speech detection in dialectal Arabic speech.A manually annotated speech dataset of 1,268 real-world voice clips was constructed and categorized into bullying, toxic, neutral, and positive classes, with substantial inter-annotator reliability (Fleiss' κ = 0.81).Furthermore, it proposes Semantic-Temporal Representation for Unified Structured Sequences (STRUSS), a lightweight hybrid GRU-LSTM network that can handle structured sequences of fixed-size acoustic representations obtained after pre-processing and log-Mel feature generation.In a representative held-out run, STRUSS achieved 98.95% accuracy with F1-scores above 98%.Across five independent runs, it achieved 99.27% ± 0.20% accuracy, outperforming Dense-only, One-dimensional Convolutional Neural Network (CNN1D), GRU1D, and LSTM1D baselines.These findings support compact acoustic modelling as a promising direction for spoken hate speech detection in dialectal and low-resource settings.
