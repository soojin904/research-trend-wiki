## 초록

This chapter traces the evolution of social media from early platforms designed to foster connection and community into highly commercialized ecosystems driven by data extraction and monetization. It explores how social networking sites gradually shifted from user-centric communication tools to sophisticated advertising and behavioral influence systems. By examining key stages in platform development, including the rise of targeted advertising, algorithmic curation, and engagement-driven design, this chapter highlights how user interactions became valuable economic assets. It also introduces the concept of users as both participants and products within platform economies. Setting the foundation for this book, this chapter frames social media as an infrastructure shaped by commercial incentives rather than neutral technological progress, preparing readers to critically examine the privacy implications that emerge throughout subsequent chapters.
