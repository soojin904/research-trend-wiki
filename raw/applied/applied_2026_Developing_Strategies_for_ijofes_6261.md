# Developing Strategies for Delivering Substance Misuse Treatment Content within a Large Rural State: Implications for Advancing Preservice and Continuing Education

## 초록

This article highlights the problem in managing substance use disorders in a rural state with sparse populations and few providers. This paper discusses the development of strategies to deliver information to increase workforce development while providing continuing education credit to participants. The participants included practitioners of mental health, substance use disorders and medical providers as well as college students. The author demonstrates several strategies for providing substance use disorder education to substantial numbers of people who may not otherwise have access to the information. Although this article focuses on the state of Alaska, the strategies developed can be generalized to other states with large rural populations or states that have populations in remote areas. Through the development of these strategies, access and awareness to substance use disorders were made available for providers to fulfil licensing requirements by expert presenters. Additionally, the delivered content positively increased attitudes for helping those experiencing addiction. For college students, this information further prepared them for employment in the field of substance misuse and the human services field.
