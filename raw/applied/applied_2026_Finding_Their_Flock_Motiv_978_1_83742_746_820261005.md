## 초록

This chapter explores the intriguing world of decoy collectors, focusing on motivations and social dynamics within Facebook groups dedicated to this niche hobby. Decoy collecting involves acquiring intricately crafted hunting decoys and is rich with historical significance and personal passion. By analyzing the content and interactions within online communities, this chapter aims to uncover the underlying reasons that drive individuals to engage in this unique form of collecting. This chapter employs a content analysis approach, including thematic examinations of posts and discussions within Facebook decoy groups. Key themes include psychological triggers of historic preservation, artistic appreciation, collection completion, and the thrill of the hunt – not for wildfowl, but for rare and often valuable decoys. Additionally, the social facet of collecting is highlighted as members express a sense of belonging and camaraderie by sharing their collections, stories, and expertise. Decoy collectors use online groups to assist others in identifying and understanding decoys, mentoring newcomers into the hobby. This analysis provides a deeper understanding of cultural and psychological factors that contribute to the allure of decoy collecting, offering insights into the broader phenomenon of hobbyist communities and online social networks.
