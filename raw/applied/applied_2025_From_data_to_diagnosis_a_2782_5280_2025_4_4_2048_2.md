# From data to diagnosis: a scoping review of NLP-based medical text summarisation methods

## 초록

Clinical documentation summarisation through Natural Language Processing (NLP) exemplifies an essential healthcare system innovation to handle growing amounts of unstructured text. The scoping review provides a mapping of the methodological field of medical text summarisation by focusing on clinical documentation and discharge summaries. Under the guidance of the accepted guidelines of scoping reviews, the literature of 2017-2025 was searched in PubMed, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Web of Science with the preset keywords. Out of 156 articles, 30 articles that were originally selected fit inclusion criteria based on automated NLP summarisation of healthcare text. The results indicate that extractive approaches prevailed in previous studies, but transformer-based models such as BERT, BART, GPT variants are currently showing better results. Assessment is mostly based on ROUGE measures, but there is little clinical validation. The main issues are a lack of data and the danger of hallucination with abstractive models and the insufficient integration of the workflow. Human-in-the-loop frameworks, standardised clinical benchmarks, and multilingual corpora development needs to be a priority of future research to enable the development of safe and effective clinical deployment.
