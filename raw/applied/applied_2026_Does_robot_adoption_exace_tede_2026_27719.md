# Does robot adoption exacerbate the fertility slump in China? Evidence from survey data

## 초록

As an integral part of automation technology, robots have significantly trans­formed the labour market structure and wage distribution, which further may also in­fluence family reproductive behaviour. As a country with a huge population, China is experiencing a severe low fertility trap. It is crucial to determine whether the widespread use of robots exacerbates this fertility decline. To investigate the effects of robots on household fertility decisions, we utilize data primarily from the China Labor-force Dy­namics Survey (CLDS) and the International Federation of Robotics (IFR). Our empirical analysis shows that increased exposure to robots significantly decreases the likelihood of childbearing in Chinese households. This result still holds across various robustness tests. Specifically, it is found that robot adoption reduces fertility through three key channels: investment in human capital, employment instability, and income inequality. These find­ings offer insights into the micro-level mechanisms that link robot adoption with fertility decisions. The conclusions provide ideas on how to ensure appropriate fertility levels in the age of artificial intelligence. First published online 28 September 2026
