## 초록

Abstrak: Perempuan prasejahtera masih menghadapi keterbatasan akses terhadap metode kontrasepsi jangka panjang (MKJP) dan deteksi dini kanker serviks. Kegiatan pengabdian ini bertujuan untuk memperluas akses layanan kesehatan reproduksi serta meningkatkan pemahaman peserta mengenai MKJP dan deteksi dini kanker serviks. Kegiatan dilaksanakan melalui penyuluhan, konseling individual, skrining kelayakan medis, pemasangan IUD atau implan, serta pemeriksaan Pap smear. Kegiatan melibatkan dua kelompok sasaran, yaitu 60 peserta edukasi MKJP dan 50 peserta edukasi deteksi dini kanker serviks. Evaluasi dilakukan melalui pre-test dan post-test lisan terstruktur yang masing-masing terdiri atas 7 pertanyaan sesuai indikator pemahaman pada setiap kelompok sasaran, serta pencatatan pemanfaatan layanan. Indikator keberhasilan kegiatan meliputi peningkatan proporsi peserta yang memahami materi setelah edukasi dan peningkatan akses pelayanan kesehatan reproduksi sesuai hasil skrining kelayakan medis. Pemahaman mengenai IUD dan implan meningkat dari 86,67% menjadi 96,67%, sedangkan pemahaman mengenai deteksi dini kanker serviks meningkat dari 24% menjadi 66%. Sebanyak 75% peserta kelompok MKJP memperoleh pelayanan IUD atau implan dan 78% peserta kelompok deteksi dini kanker serviks menjalani pemeriksaan Pap smear. Pelayanan kesehatan reproduksi terpadu melalui edukasi, konseling, skrining, dan pelayanan klinis mampu meningkatkan pemahaman serta memperluas akses layanan kesehatan reproduksi perempuan prasejahtera. Edukasi deteksi dini kanker serviks tetap perlu dilakukan secara berulang dan berkelanjutan untuk memperkuat kesadaran peserta.Abstract: Women from low-income households continue to face limited access to long-acting reversible contraception (LARC) and early cervical cancer detection. This community service program aimed to expand reproductive health access and improve participants’ understanding of LARC and cervical cancer screening. The activities included health education, individual counseling, medical eligibility screening, IUD or implant insertion, and Pap smear examinations. The program involved two groups consisting of 60 participants receiving LARC education and 50 participants receiving cervical cancer early-detection education. Evaluation was conducted through structured oral pre-test and post-test consisting of 7 questions for each group based on the educational indicators, along with documentation of service utilization. The success indicators included increased participant understanding after education and improved access to reproductive health services based on medical eligibility screening. Understanding of IUD and implant methods increased from 86.67% to 96.67%, while understanding of early cervical cancer detection increased from 24% to 66%. A total of 75% of participants in the LARC group received IUD or implant services, and 78% of participants in the cervical cancer detection group underwent Pap smear examinations. Integrated reproductive health services combining education, counseling, screening, and clinical services improved understanding and access to reproductive health services among low-income women. Continuous education is needed to strengthen awareness of early cervical cancer detection.
