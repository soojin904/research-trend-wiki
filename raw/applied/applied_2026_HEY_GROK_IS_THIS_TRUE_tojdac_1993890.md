# HEY @GROK, IS THIS TRUE? AN EXAMINATION OF NEWS VERIFICATION PRACTICES ON X

**저자**: Mesut YILMAZ

## 초록

AbstractGenerative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) systems have become a new actor in news verification practices. This study aims to examine the news verification requests that Turkish-speaking users on Platform X make to Grok, as well as Grok’s role in the news verification process. In the study, 276 news verification requests directed at Grok on the X platform during June 2026 were analyzed using qualitative content analysis. As part of the analysis, the verification requests were evaluated based on news topic, content type, news source, account verification status, account verification badge, whether the news was national or international, and Grok’s responses. The findings indicate that Grok can respond quickly to news verification requests and adapt to the user’s communication style, meticulously detailing the verification process. The news topics with the highest number of verification requests were, in order of sports, politics, and crime news, and explanatory content was verified more than other content types. It was also determined that the vast majority of accounts requesting news verification were anonymous, that verification requests covered both national and international news, and that users tended to trust Grok, as they requested verification from it regardless of whether the content was shared by an official organization. In the study, Grok is evaluated as an AI-based verification tool that supports the news verification ecosystem on the X platform, facilitates users’ information verification practices, and functions effectively through the Community Notes mechanism.Keywords: Large Language Models, Generative Artificial Intelligence, X, Grok.
