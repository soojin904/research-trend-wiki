## 초록

This chapter serves as an anchor for this edited book. It provides the book’s aim, its significance, and its argument. This book aims to examine the state managing digital governance in Southeast Asia, especially about internet control and democracy. This book argues that the internet, especially the social media, has two sides of a coin: it can spur economic development and democracy; on the other hand, it can destroy democracy and social and political stability if it is not used properly. This book offers diverse methods for gathering the data. Some employ primary research such as collecting the data through in-depth and elite interviews, social network analysis (SNA), while others resort to secondary research such as gathering the data from journals, books, or government and academic reports. Few studies examine internet control and democracy in Southeast Asia, and those that do have not explored the topic in sufficient depth. This book offers 11 case studies (from nine countries); thus it is the first book in the field of digital governance that provides the widest coverage of the topic in the region.
