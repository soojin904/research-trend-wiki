# Penggunaan Discord Dalam Meningkatkan Komunikasi Dan Interaksi Sosial Game Online (Studi Kasus Remaja Pemain Game Online di Kabupaten Bandung)

**저자**: Mochamad Shiddik Al Farizi; Yogie Alontari; Dadan Saputra; Saiful Hadi

**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.62379/jmc.v3i1.1877
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study aims to analyze communication patterns, social interaction, and the utilization of Discord in improving communication and social interaction among adolescent online game players in Bandung Regency. This study employed a qualitative approach using a case study method. The research participants consisted of four informants selected through purposive sampling. Data were collected through interviews, observation, and documentation, and were analyzed using the interactive model of Miles, Huberman, and Saldana, with data validity examined through source triangulation and technique triangulation. The results showed that Discord is used as a communication medium through the Voice Channel and Text Channel features, both for coordination during online gaming activities and for communication beyond gaming. Intensive and continuous interactions developed from relationships among players into friendships, characterized by regular communication, sharing experiences, mutual support, and the development of social closeness. The utilization of Discord also facilitates communication, increases users' confidence in communicating, expands social networks, and supports communication in daily life. These findings were analyzed using Joseph B. Walther's Social Information Processing (SIP) Theory, which explains that interpersonal relationships can be established through digital communication that occurs intensively and continuously.
