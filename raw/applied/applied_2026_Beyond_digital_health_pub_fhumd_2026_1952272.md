# Beyond digital health: public and community digital interventions for migrant psychosocial well-being in Spain and Argentina

**출처**: Frontiers in Human Dynamics, Vol.8
**발행일**: 2026-10-02

**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Introduction The rapid digitalization of services for migrant populations has transformed the ways in which information, guidance, and social support are delivered. However, existing research has primarily focused on clinical digital mental health interventions or on migrants' own uses of digital technologies, paying considerably less attention to digital devices developed by public institutions and community organizations. This study comparatively analyzes four digital intervention devices implemented in Spain and Argentina, two public and two community-based, to examine how they may contribute to psychosocial well-being from a social determinants of health perspective. Methods and Results Using a qualitative design based on digital ethnography and comparative thematic content analysis, the study explores the characteristics, functions, and intervention strategies of these digital resources, with particular attention to their roles in information provision, guidance, psychosocial support, and community participation. The findings show that these digital resources function as hybrid intervention ecosystems that integrate technological tools, professional support, and community networks rather than merely providing information. Four predominant intervention models were identified: administrative-information, institutional guidance and service access, rights-based community, and community guidance, accompaniment, and referral. Discussion Together, these models offer a novel analytical framework for understanding the diverse roles of digital interventions in migration contexts. Rather than providing direct therapeutic care, these devices contribute to psychosocial well-being by reducing barriers to access, strengthening social support networks, promoting rights, and facilitating connections with specialized services. By shifting the focus from clinical digital health to digital social intervention ecosystems, this study expands current debates on digital interventions for migrant populations and provides practical implications for the development of more inclusive, accessible, and culturally responsive digital resources aimed at promoting psychosocial well-being.
