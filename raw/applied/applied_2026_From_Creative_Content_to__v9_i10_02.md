# From Creative Content to Commercial Value: Determinants of Financial Viability in Student-Led Publishing Startups

**저자**: Quan Nguyen Van; Thuy Nguyen Thanh
**출처**: Journal of Economics Finance and Management Studies, Vol.09 No.10, pp.5124-5141
**발행일**: 2026-10-03
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.47191/jefms/v9-i10-02
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

This study examines the determinants of financial viability in student-led publishing startups in Vietnam by investigating how creative and market-oriented attributes contribute to perceived commercialization capability and, subsequently, financial viability. Drawing on the Resource-Based View and Dynamic Capabilities View, the study develops a model incorporating content quality, visual design, community engagement, brand authenticity, and digital accessibility as antecedents of perceived commercialization capability. Data were collected from 217 respondents involved in student-led publishing ventures and analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM). The findings indicate that all five antecedents positively influence perceived commercialization capability, while perceived commercialization capability is strongly associated with financial viability. The results also demonstrate significant indirect relationships between all five antecedents and financial viability through perceived commercialization capability. The findings highlight the importance of developing commercialization capabilities alongside creative resources and suggest that student publishing ventures should integrate content development, brand building, community engagement, and digital distribution with systematic market-oriented activities. The study contributes to the literature by connecting creative value, commercialization capability, and financial viability within the context of student-led publishing ventures in Vietnam.
