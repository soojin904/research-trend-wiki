## 초록

This paper examines the theoretical foundations, determinants and policy implications of women's entrepreneurship through an integrated economic and institutional lens. Drawing on classical entrepreneurship theory, human capital theory, institutional economics, resource-based perspectives and the gender-aware 5M framework, the study explores how entrepreneurial agency interacts with financial access, skills, social networks, care responsibilities and the wider regulatory environment.
