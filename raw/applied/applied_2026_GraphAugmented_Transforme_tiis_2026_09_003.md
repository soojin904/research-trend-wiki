# Graph-Augmented Transformer Fusion (GA-TFusion) Model for Sentiment Analysis of Online Product Reviews Using Emoji Embeddings

**저자**: Nimmi M K; P Amudha
**출처**: KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems, Vol.20 No.9
**발행일**: 2026-09-30
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.3837/tiis.2026.09.003
**수집 키워드**: text analysis

## 초록

Online product reviews are often short, written in multiple languages, and filled with emojis.While emojis carry strong emotional meaning, most traditional sentiment analysis models ignore them, leading to the omission of important information.This research presented a deeplearning-assisted Graph-Augmented Transformer Fusion (GA-TFusion) model that uses emoji information for sentiment analysis.The model first cleans and normalises emoji sequences, then builds an emoji co-occurrence graph and uses Graph Convolution to capture relationships between emojis.Emoji features are fused through a projection layer and trained together for classification and rating prediction.This research created a mixed dataset using informal reviews from Meesho, detailed fashion reviews from Myntra, and labelled emoji sentiment data from Kaggle.Results show that GA-TFusion achieves 94.9% accuracy, 0.948 F1-score, and 0.989 ROC-AUC, outperforming models that use only text or only emojis.The model also maintains high precision (0.975) and recall (0.923), demonstrating its ability to handle noisy reviews and emoji usage effectively.This research shows that treating emojis as important semantic features improves sentiment analysis and offers valuable insights for e-commerce platforms.Future work will extend this model to larger multilingual datasets and explore multimodal signals, such as images and stickers, to strengthen sentiment understanding.
