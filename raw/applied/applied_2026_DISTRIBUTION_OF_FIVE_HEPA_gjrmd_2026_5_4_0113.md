# DISTRIBUTION OF FIVE HEPATITIS B VIRUS MARKERS IN HIV INFECTED AND UNINFECTED INJECTING DRUG USERS IN COASTAL KENYA

**저자**: Mark Kilongosi Webale
**출처**: Global journal of research in medicine and dentistry, Vol.5 No.4, pp.085-092
**발행일**: 2026-10-09
**DOI**: https://doi.org/10.58175/gjrmd.2026.5.4.0113
**수집 키워드**: social network

## 초록

Aims: This study determined HBV sero-markers profile in HIV-1 infected (n=157) and uninfected (n=214) IDUs from Mombasa, Kenya HIV-1 diagnosis was performed using DetermineTM and UnigoldTM rapid tests. Methodology: The CD4+ T cells were enumerated using the FacsCalibur flow cytometer and categorized according to the Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) immunological staging criteria. HBV seroprofile was determined using HBV-5 panel rapid test cassettes. Clinical staging was based on serological profile of the five HBV markers. Results: HBsAg (P=0.004) and HBcAb-IgM (P=0.008) positivities were higher in the HIV-1 infected compared to the uninfected individuals while the opposite was observed for HBsAb positivity (P=0.019). Rates of acute (P=0.033) and chronic (P=0.021) clinical stages were higher in the HIV-1 infected compared to the uninfected individuals but the inverse was noticed for vaccine type response rate (P=0.008). Only genotype A, sub-genotype A1 was detected with higher frequencies noted in the HIV-1 infected (n=12) compared to the uninfected (n=4) individuals. Conclusion: HBsAg and HBcAb-IgM sero-markers and acute and chronic clinical categories are higher while HBsAb sero-marker and vaccine type response stage are lower in HIV-1 infected compared to uninfected IDUs at Mombasa, Kenya. Taken together, HIV infected injection drug users are at risk of acquiring HBV and progressing to chronic infection.
