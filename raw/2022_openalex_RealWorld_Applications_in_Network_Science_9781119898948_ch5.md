# Real‐World Applications in Network Science

## 초록

This chapter presents some use cases where network analysis and network optimization algorithms were used to solve a particular problem, or at least were used as part of an overall solution. The first two cases were created to demonstrate some of the network optimization capabilities, providing practical examples on using network algorithms to understand a particular scenario. The remaining cases focus more on the network analysis algorithms, looking into multiple types of subgraph detection, centrality metrics calculation, and ultimately correlation analysis between those outcomes from some specific business events like churn, default, fraud, or consumption and the customer's profile in terms of social structures. Social network analysis often raises relevant knowledge about the customers' behaviors, particularly in relation to their relations within the social structures, which is highly correlated to the influence effect of business events over time.
